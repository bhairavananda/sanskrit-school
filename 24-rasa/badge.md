# Stage 25 Badge: Rasavit

**Title:** Rasa Artist
**Badge:** 🏅 Rasavit

## Requirements

Compose 3 short verses, each in a different rasa (e.g., śṛṅgāra, karuṇa, vīra). Explain how word choice creates the rasa.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post with commentary

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 26 content and grants the **Rasavit** role on Discord.

# Stage 29 Badge: Niṣiddhākṣarīkāra

**Title:** Letter Dodger
**Badge:** 🏅 Niṣiddhākṣarīkāra

## Requirements

Compose a 4-line verse that completely avoids a given letter (e.g., no 'na' or no 'ta').

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post — letter given by moderator

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 30 content and grants the **Niṣiddhākṣarīkāra** role on Discord.

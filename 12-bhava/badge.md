# Stage 13 Badge: Bhāvavit

**Title:** Emotion Speaker
**Badge:** 🏅 Bhāvavit

## Requirements

Express 5 distinct emotions (joy, sorrow, devotion, wonder, peace) in 2-3 Sanskrit sentences each — no English whatsoever.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text or voice post

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 14 content and grants the **Bhāvavit** role on Discord.

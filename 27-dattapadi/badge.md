# Stage 28 Badge: Dattapadīkāra

**Title:** Forced-Word Poet
**Badge:** 🏅 Dattapadīkāra

## Requirements

Compose a verse incorporating 4 given words (chosen by moderator) naturally into a metrically correct śloka.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post — words given by moderator

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 29 content and grants the **Dattapadīkāra** role on Discord.

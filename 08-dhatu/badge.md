# Stage 9 Badge: Dhātuvit

**Title:** Root Seer
**Badge:** 🏅 Dhātuvit

## Requirements

1. Given 5 Sanskrit words, identify the dhātu + upasarga + pratyaya
2. From one dhātu (e.g., √gam), derive 5 different words using different prefixes
3. Explain the dhātu behind 3 deity names

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 10 content and grants the **Dhātuvit** role on Discord.

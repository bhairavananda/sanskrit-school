# Stage 32 Badge: Dhāraṇāvit II

**Title:** Recall Artist
**Badge:** 🏅 Dhāraṇāvit II

## Requirements

After composing a verse, wait 10 minutes, then recall and recite it from a single cue word.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Live in voice channel

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 33 content and grants the **Dhāraṇāvit II** role on Discord.

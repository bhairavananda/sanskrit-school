# Stage 16 Badge: Prārthanāsiddha

**Title:** Prayer Composer
**Badge:** 🏅 Prārthanāsiddha

## Requirements

Write and recite an original Sanskrit prayer of 6+ lines using imperatives, vocatives, and at least 3 different vibhaktis.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Voice note + text

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 17 content and grants the **Prārthanāsiddha** role on Discord.

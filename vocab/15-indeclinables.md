# 15 — Indeclinables (Avyayas)

These particles, conjunctions, and adverbs appear constantly. Mastering them is essential for reading any of the five texts fluently.

Source key: DM = Devī Māhātmyam, LS = Lalitā Sahasranāma, KKS = Kakārādi Kālī SN, KS = Kālī SN, VS = Viṣṇu SN

# 18 — Recurring Phrases, Refrains, and Colophons

These fixed expressions appear dozens or hundreds of times across the texts. Recognize them instantly.

Source key: DM = Devī Māhātmyam, LS = Lalitā Sahasranāma, KKS = Kakārādi Kālī SN, KS = Kālī SN, VS = Viṣṇu SN

---

## A. DM Narrative Markers

| Phrase | Meaning | Frequency |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| ṛṣir uvāca | the sage said | every chapter (narrator switch) |
| rājovāca | the king said | DM 1.3, 9.1, 13.1 |
| devyuvāca | the Goddess said | DM 5.116, 6.10, 12.1 |
| devā ūcuḥ | the gods said | DM 4.1, 5.9 |
| mārkaṇḍeya uvāca | Mārkaṇḍeya said | DM 13.6, 13.26 |

## B. DM Transitional Phrases

| Phrase | Meaning | Usage |
|--------|---------|-------|
| tataḥ | then, thereupon | most common connector (~100 times) |
| ity uktvā | having said thus | speech→action transition |
| iti śrutvā | having heard thus | hearing→response |
| evaṃ stutvā | having praised thus | stuti→result |
| tān/tāṃ dṛṣṭvā | having seen them/her | vision→action |
| tato devī | then the Goddess | action pivot |

## D. DM Ch.5 — Yā Devī Refrain

| Phrase | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| yā devī sarvabhūteṣu X-rūpeṇa saṃsthitā | She who in all beings is established as X |
| namaḥ tasyai namaḥ tasyai namaḥ tasyai namo namaḥ | Salutation to her, to her, to her — salutations! |

X rotates through: viṣṇumāyā, cetanā, buddhi, nidrā, kṣudhā, chāyā, śakti, tṛṣṇā, kṣānti, jāti, lajjā, śānti, śraddhā, kānti, lakṣmī, vṛtti, smṛti, dayā, tuṣṭi, mātṛ, bhrānti (DM 5.14-78)

## E. DM Ch.11 — Nārāyaṇī Stuti Refrain

| Phrase | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| nārāyaṇi namo'stu te | O Nārāyaṇī, salutations to you |

Appears after nearly every verse in Ch.11 as a recurring refrain.

## G. DM Colophon Formula

| Phrase | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| iti śrīmārkaṇḍeyapurāṇe sāvarṇike manvantare devīmāhātmye | Thus in the Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa, in the Sāvarṇika Manvantara, in the Devī Māhātmya |
| X-vadho nāma Y-o'dhyāyaḥ | named "the slaying of X", the Y-th chapter |

## H. Sahasranāma Opening/Closing Formulae

### Namāvalī Format
| Phrase | Meaning | Text |
|--------|---------|------|
| oṃ X-āya/ai namaḥ | Oṃ, salutation to X | VS/LS/KKS/KS throughout |
| oṃ X-āya namaḥ | (masculine dative) | VS throughout |
| oṃ X-yai namaḥ | (feminine dative) | LS/KKS/KS throughout |

### VS Opening
| Phrase | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| śrī bhagavān uvāca | the blessed Lord said |
| yaḥ sarvatrānavadyātmā | he who is the blameless self everywhere |

### LS Opening (Pūrvabhāga)
| Phrase | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| hayagrīva uvāca | Hayagrīva said |
| agastyāya varaṃ dattvā | having given a boon to Agastya |

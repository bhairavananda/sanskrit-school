# Deity Vibhakti Tables — The Eight Baseplates

Every noun stem type in Sanskrit is a different LEGO baseplate. Learn one canonical deity per baseplate and you can decline any noun that shares that ending.

Each deity below is the **model word** for its stem type. Memorize these eight tables and you cover the vast majority of Sanskrit nouns.

---

## 1. Rāma — a-kārānta puṃliṅga (masculine a-stem)

The most common stem type. Covers: devaḥ, śivaḥ, kṛṣṇaḥ, gaṇeśaḥ, sūryaḥ, candraḥ, and thousands more.

| Vibhakti | Ekavacana | Dvivacana | Bahuvacana |
|----------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Prathamā | rāmaḥ | rāmau | rāmāḥ |
| Dvitīyā | rāmam | rāmau | rāmān |
| Tṛtīyā | rāmeṇa | rāmābhyām | rāmaiḥ |
| Caturthī | rāmāya | rāmābhyām | rāmebhyaḥ |
| Pañcamī | rāmāt | rāmābhyām | rāmebhyaḥ |
| Ṣaṣṭhī | rāmasya | rāmayoḥ | rāmāṇām |
| Saptamī | rāme | rāmayoḥ | rāmeṣu |
| Sambodhana | he rāma! | he rāmau! | he rāmāḥ! |

> **Mantra test:** rāmāya namaḥ (dative) — "salutation to Rāma"
> **Build:** rāma + āya = rāmāya (snap the caturthī brick)

---

## 2. Durgā — ā-kārānta strīliṅga (feminine ā-stem)

Covers: gaṅgā, sītā, rādhā, mālā, latā, pūjā, vidyā, and all feminine -ā nouns.

| Vibhakti | Ekavacana | Dvivacana | Bahuvacana |
|----------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Prathamā | durgā | durge | durgāḥ |
| Dvitīyā | durgām | durge | durgāḥ |
| Tṛtīyā | durgayā | durgābhyām | durgābhiḥ |
| Caturthī | durgāyai | durgābhyām | durgābhyaḥ |
| Pañcamī | durgāyāḥ | durgābhyām | durgābhyaḥ |
| Ṣaṣṭhī | durgāyāḥ | durgayoḥ | durgāṇām |
| Saptamī | durgāyām | durgayoḥ | durgāsu |
| Sambodhana | he durge! | he durge! | he durgāḥ! |

> **Mantra test:** durgāyai namaḥ (dative) — "salutation to Durgā"
> **Notice:** The ā-plate has unique feminine connectors: -āyai, -āyāḥ, -āyām

---

## 3. Hari — i-kārānta puṃliṅga (masculine i-stem)

Covers: agniḥ, muniḥ, kaviḥ, ṛṣiḥ, rāśiḥ, and all masculine -i nouns.

| Vibhakti | Ekavacana | Dvivacana | Bahuvacana |
|----------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Prathamā | hariḥ | harī | harayaḥ |
| Dvitīyā | harim | harī | harīn |
| Tṛtīyā | hariṇā | haribhyām | haribhiḥ |
| Caturthī | haraye | haribhyām | haribhyaḥ |
| Pañcamī | hareḥ | haribhyām | haribhyaḥ |
| Ṣaṣṭhī | hareḥ | haryoḥ | harīṇām |
| Saptamī | harau | haryoḥ | hariṣu |
| Sambodhana | he hare! | he harī! | he harayaḥ! |

> **Mantra test:** haraye namaḥ (dative) — "salutation to Hari"
> **Notice:** Pañcamī and ṣaṣṭhī are identical (hareḥ). The -i plate has this quirk.

---

## 4. Lakṣmī — ī-kārānta strīliṅga (feminine ī-stem)

Covers: devī, nadī, pārvatī, sarasvatī, gaurī, and all feminine -ī nouns.

| Vibhakti | Ekavacana | Dvivacana | Bahuvacana |
|----------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Prathamā | lakṣmīḥ | lakṣmyau | lakṣmyaḥ |
| Dvitīyā | lakṣmīm | lakṣmyau | lakṣmīḥ |
| Tṛtīyā | lakṣmyā | lakṣmībhyām | lakṣmībhiḥ |
| Caturthī | lakṣmyai | lakṣmībhyām | lakṣmībhyaḥ |
| Pañcamī | lakṣmyāḥ | lakṣmībhyām | lakṣmībhyaḥ |
| Ṣaṣṭhī | lakṣmyāḥ | lakṣmyoḥ | lakṣmīṇām |
| Saptamī | lakṣmyām | lakṣmyoḥ | lakṣmīṣu |
| Sambodhana | he lakṣmi! | he lakṣmyau! | he lakṣmyaḥ! |

> **Mantra test:** lakṣmyai namaḥ (dative) — "salutation to Lakṣmī"
> **Notice:** The ī shortens before consonant-initial endings (-bhyām), and becomes -y- before vowel-initial endings

---

## 5. Viṣṇu — u-kārānta puṃliṅga (masculine u-stem)

Covers: guruḥ, śatruḥ, paśuḥ, vāyuḥ, śambhuḥ, bhānuḥ, and all masculine -u nouns.

| Vibhakti | Ekavacana | Dvivacana | Bahuvacana |
|----------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Prathamā | viṣṇuḥ | viṣṇū | viṣṇavaḥ |
| Dvitīyā | viṣṇum | viṣṇū | viṣṇūn |
| Tṛtīyā | viṣṇunā | viṣṇubhyām | viṣṇubhiḥ |
| Caturthī | viṣṇave | viṣṇubhyām | viṣṇubhyaḥ |
| Pañcamī | viṣṇoḥ | viṣṇubhyām | viṣṇubhyaḥ |
| Ṣaṣṭhī | viṣṇoḥ | viṣṇvoḥ | viṣṇūnām |
| Saptamī | viṣṇau | viṣṇvoḥ | viṣṇuṣu |
| Sambodhana | he viṣṇo! | he viṣṇū! | he viṣṇavaḥ! |

> **Mantra test:** viṣṇave namaḥ (dative) — "salutation to Viṣṇu"
> **Notice:** The u-plate mirrors the i-plate pattern. Pañcamī = ṣaṣṭhī (viṣṇoḥ). Sambodhana guṇa → viṣṇo!

---

## 6. Bhagavat — at-kārānta puṃliṅga (consonant stem, present participle type)

Covers: mahāt, vidvat, and all -at/-mat/-vat stems (dhīmat, śrīmat, hanumāt).

| Vibhakti | Ekavacana | Dvivacana | Bahuvacana |
|----------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Prathamā | bhagavān | bhagavantau | bhagavantaḥ |
| Dvitīyā | bhagavantam | bhagavantau | bhagavataḥ |
| Tṛtīyā | bhagavatā | bhagavadbhyām | bhagavadbhiḥ |
| Caturthī | bhagavate | bhagavadbhyām | bhagavadbhyaḥ |
| Pañcamī | bhagavataḥ | bhagavadbhyām | bhagavadbhyaḥ |
| Ṣaṣṭhī | bhagavataḥ | bhagavatoḥ | bhagavatām |
| Saptamī | bhagavati | bhagavatoḥ | bhagavatsu |
| Sambodhana | he bhagavan! | he bhagavantau! | he bhagavantaḥ! |

> **Mantra test:** bhagavate namaḥ (dative) — "salutation to the Blessed One"
> **Notice:** Prathamā singular gets a special strong form: bhagavat → bhagavān (the -t drops and the vowel lengthens). This is the "upgrade brick."

---

## 7. Ātman — an-kārānta puṃliṅga (n-stem)

Covers: brahman, rājan, and all -an stems.

| Vibhakti | Ekavacana | Dvivacana | Bahuvacana |
|----------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Prathamā | ātmā | ātmānau | ātmānaḥ |
| Dvitīyā | ātmānam | ātmānau | ātmanaḥ |
| Tṛtīyā | ātmanā | ātmabhyām | ātmabhiḥ |
| Caturthī | ātmane | ātmabhyām | ātmabhyaḥ |
| Pañcamī | ātmanaḥ | ātmabhyām | ātmabhyaḥ |
| Ṣaṣṭhī | ātmanaḥ | ātmanoḥ | ātmanām |
| Saptamī | ātmani | ātmanoḥ | ātmasu |
| Sambodhana | he ātman! | he ātmānau! | he ātmānaḥ! |

> **Mantra test:** ātmane namaḥ (dative) — "salutation to the Self"
> **Notice:** The n-stem has three grades — strong (ātmān-), middle (ātman-), weak (ātma-). Prathamā/dvitīyā use strong, the rest use middle or weak.

---

## 8. Mātṛ — ṛ-kārānta strīliṅga (feminine ṛ-stem)

Covers all -tṛ agent nouns and kinship terms: pitṛ (father), bhartṛ (husband/sustainer), kartṛ (doer), dātṛ (giver), dhātṛ (creator), svasṛ (sister). Mātṛ serves double duty — it is both a kinship term and, as a title of Devī, a sacred name.

The ṛ-stem is special: it has **three stem grades** like the n-stem:
- **Strong** (vṛddhi): mātār- (prathamā/dvitīyā/sambodhana singular & dual)
- **Middle** (guṇa): mātar- (before consonant endings in some forms)
- **Weak** (zero): mātr- (before vowel-initial endings)

### Feminine ṛ-stem (mātṛ)

| Vibhakti | Ekavacana | Dvivacana | Bahuvacana |
|----------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Prathamā | mātā | mātarau | mātaraḥ |
| Dvitīyā | mātaram | mātarau | mātṝḥ |
| Tṛtīyā | mātrā | mātṛbhyām | mātṛbhiḥ |
| Caturthī | mātre | mātṛbhyām | mātṛbhyaḥ |
| Pañcamī | mātuḥ | mātṛbhyām | mātṛbhyaḥ |
| Ṣaṣṭhī | mātuḥ | mātroḥ | mātṝṇām |
| Saptamī | mātari | mātroḥ | mātṛṣu |
| Sambodhana | he mātaḥ! | he mātarau! | he mātaraḥ! |

> **Mantra test:** mātre namaḥ (dative) — "salutation to the Mother"
> **Notice:** Pañcamī = ṣaṣṭhī (mātuḥ) — same quirk as the i-plate and u-plate. The prathamā singular drops the -ṛ entirely → mātā (like ātmā on the n-plate). Bahuvacana dvitīyā uses the rare long ṝ: mātṝḥ.

### Masculine ṛ-stem (pitṛ) — for comparison

The masculine ṛ-stem differs in prathamā/dvitīyā/tṛtīyā singular:

| Vibhakti | Ekavacana | Dvivacana | Bahuvacana |
|----------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Prathamā | pitā | pitarau | pitaraḥ |
| Dvitīyā | pitaram | pitarau | pitṝn |
| Tṛtīyā | pitrā | pitṛbhyām | pitṛbhiḥ |
| Caturthī | pitre | pitṛbhyām | pitṛbhyaḥ |
| Pañcamī | pituḥ | pitṛbhyām | pitṛbhyaḥ |
| Ṣaṣṭhī | pituḥ | pitroḥ | pitṝṇām |
| Saptamī | pitari | pitroḥ | pitṛṣu |

> **Build insight:** kartṛ (doer), dātṛ (giver), dhātṛ (creator) — all the -tṛ agent nouns from dhātu + tṛc pratyaya follow this same plate. So when you snap the -tṛ suffix onto any root, you already know every form it can take.

## The Pattern Across Baseplates

All eight baseplates share the same skeleton. The vibhakti *meaning* never changes — only the connector shape does:

| Vibhakti | Meaning | a-plate | ā-plate | i-plate | ī-plate | u-plate | ṛ-plate |
|----------|---------|---------|---------|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| Prathamā | subject | -aḥ | -ā | -iḥ | -īḥ | -uḥ | -ā |
| Dvitīyā | object | -am | -ām | -im | -īm | -um | -aram |
| Tṛtīyā | by/with | -ena | -ayā | -iṇā | -yā | -unā | -rā |
| Caturthī | for/to | -āya | -āyai | -aye | -yai | -ave | -re |
| Pañcamī | from | -āt | -āyāḥ | -eḥ | -yāḥ | -oḥ | -uḥ |
| Ṣaṣṭhī | of | -asya | -āyāḥ | -eḥ | -yāḥ | -oḥ | -uḥ |
| Saptamī | in/on | -e | -āyām | -au | -yām | -au | -ari |

> The function is fixed. The shape varies by baseplate. Learn the baseplate, apply it to any noun with that ending.

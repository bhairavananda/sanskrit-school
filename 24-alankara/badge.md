# Stage 24 Badge: Alaṅkāravit

**Title:** Ornament Master
**Badge:** 🏅 Alaṅkāravit

## Requirements

Compose a verse using 3 distinct alaṅkāras (e.g., upamā, rūpaka, anuprāsa). Identify each ornament.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post with annotation

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 25 content and grants the **Alaṅkāravit** role on Discord.

# Stage 5 Badge: Rūpavit

**Title:** Baseplate Master
**Badge:** 🏅 Rūpavit

## Requirements

1. Decline one noun from 3 different baseplates (e.g., Rāma, Durgā, Viṣṇu) through all 8 vibhaktis × 3 vacanas
2. Identify the baseplate for 5 given nouns
3. Decline one sarvānāma (tad or kim) in all 3 genders

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post with tables

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 6 content and grants the **Rūpavit** role on Discord.

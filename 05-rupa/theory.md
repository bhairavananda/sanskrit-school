# Stage 3: Rūpa — Case, Number, and Gender (Declension)

## Goal
Learn to transform nouns through all cases (vibhaktis), numbers, and genders. Decline devotional vocabulary actively so that words become usable in sentences.

## What is Rūpa?

**Rūpa** (रूप) means "form." In Sanskrit, nouns change their form (ending) depending on their role in a sentence. This system of changes is called **declension** (śabda-rūpa).

## The Eight Cases (Vibhaktis)

| # | Vibhakti | Name | Function | Question | Example |
|---|---------|------|----------|----------|---------|
| 1 | Prathamā | Nominative | Subject | Who/What? | śivaḥ (Śiva [does]) |
| 2 | Dvitīyā | Accusative | Object | Whom/What? | śivam (Śiva [I worship]) |
| 3 | Tṛtīyā | Instrumental | By/With | By whom? | śivena (by Śiva) |
| 4 | Caturthī | Dative | For/To | For whom? | śivāya (for Śiva) |
| 5 | Pañcamī | Ablative | From | From whom? | śivāt (from Śiva) |
| 6 | Ṣaṣṭhī | Genitive | Of | Whose? | śivasya (of Śiva) |
| 7 | Saptamī | Locative | In/On | Where? | śive (in Śiva) |
| 8 | Sambodhana | Vocative | Address | O...! | he śiva! |

## The Three Numbers

| Vacana | Meaning | Example (prathamā of deva) |
|--------|---------|---------------------------|
| Ekavacana | Singular | devaḥ |
| Dvivacana | Dual | devau |
| Bahuvacana | Plural | devāḥ |

| Vibhakti | Eka | Dvi | Bahu |
|----------|-----|-----|------|
| Prathamā | devaḥ | devau | devāḥ |
| Dvitīyā | devam | devau | devān |
| Tṛtīyā | devena | devābhyām | devaiḥ |
| Caturthī | devāya | devābhyām | devebhyaḥ |
| Pañcamī | devāt | devābhyām | devebhyaḥ |
| Ṣaṣṭhī | devasya | devayoḥ | devānām |
| Saptamī | deve | devayoḥ | deveṣu |
| Sambodhana | he deva! | he devau! | he devāḥ! |

## Devotional Context: "Transform the Deity"

See how a single name changes across cases:

| Case | Form | Devotional Sentence |
|------|------|-------------------|
| Prathamā | śivaḥ | śivaḥ asti — Śiva exists |
| Dvitīyā | śivam | śivam namāmi — I bow to Śiva |
| Tṛtīyā | śivena | śivena rakṣitaḥ — protected by Śiva |
| Caturthī | śivāya | śivāya namaḥ — salutation to Śiva |
| Pañcamī | śivāt | śivāt āgataḥ — come from Śiva |
| Ṣaṣṭhī | śivasya | śivasya kṛpā — Śiva's grace |
| Saptamī | śive | śive bhaktiḥ — devotion in/to Śiva |
| Sambodhana | he śiva! | he śiva! prasīda — O Śiva! be gracious |

## Key Insight

The case system is what makes Sanskrit a free-word-order language. Since the function of each word is encoded in its ending, you can rearrange words freely:
- śivāya puṣpam dadāmi
- puṣpam śivāya dadāmi
- dadāmi śivāya puṣpam

All three mean: "I give a flower to Śiva."

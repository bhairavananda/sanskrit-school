# Stage 35 Badge: Aṣṭāvadhānī

**Title:** Eight-Fold Attention
**Badge:** 🏅 Aṣṭāvadhānī

## Requirements

Complete a simulated Aṣṭāvadhāna: hold 8 simultaneous challenges (samasyāpūraṇa, dattapadī, niṣiddhākṣarī, āśu-kavitva, nyasta-akṣarī, aprastuta-prasaṅga, purāṇa-paṭhana, gaṇanā).

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Live performance in voice stage — panel of 8 questioners

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 36 content and grants the **Aṣṭāvadhānī** role on Discord.

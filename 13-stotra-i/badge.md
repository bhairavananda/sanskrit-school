# Stage 14 Badge: Stutikāra I

**Title:** First Praise
**Badge:** 🏅 Stutikāra I

## Requirements

Compose and post an original 4-line praise verse in Sanskrit for your iṣṭadevatā. Must use correct accusative (dvitīyā) constructions.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post with Devanāgarī + IAST

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 15 content and grants the **Stutikāra I** role on Discord.

# Stage 26 Badge: Darśanavit

**Title:** Philosopher-Poet
**Badge:** 🏅 Darśanavit

## Requirements

Express a Vedāntic or Tāntric concept as a 4-line verse. Post with a prose explanation of the philosophical content.

**Format:** Text post

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 27 content and grants the **Darśanavit** role on Discord.

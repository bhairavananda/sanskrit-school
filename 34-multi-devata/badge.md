# Stage 34 Badge: Bahudevatāvit

**Title:** Deity Switcher
**Badge:** 🏅 Bahudevatāvit

## Requirements

Compose verses for 3 different deities in rapid succession (moderator calls the deity). Each must use deity-specific vocabulary and appropriate rasa.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Live in voice channel

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 35 content and grants the **Bahudevatāvit** role on Discord.

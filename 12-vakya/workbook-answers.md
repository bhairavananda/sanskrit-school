## Section E

**E1.** Stem identification:
1. **manasā** — stem: manas, type: neuter -as stem, instrumental singular
2. **tapaḥ** — stem: tapas, type: neuter -as stem, nominative/accusative singular
3. **vāk** — stem: vāc, type: feminine root consonant stem, nominative singular (c → k at word-end)
4. **tejasā** — stem: tejas, type: neuter -as stem, instrumental singular
5. **haviḥ** — stem: havis, type: neuter -is stem, nominative/accusative singular
6. **jyotiḥ** — stem: jyotis, type: neuter -is stem, nominative/accusative singular
7. **āyuḥ** — stem: āyus, type: neuter -us stem, nominative/accusative singular
8. **cakṣuḥ** — stem: cakṣus, type: neuter -us stem, nominative/accusative singular
9. **vācam** — stem: vāc, type: feminine root consonant stem, accusative singular
10. **manāṃsi** — stem: manas, type: neuter -as stem, nominative/accusative plural

**E2.**
1. **Instrumental singular:**
   - manas → **manasā** (-s- remains as -s- after -a-)
   - havis → **haviṣā** (-s- becomes -ṣ- after -i-, retroflexion rule)
   - cakṣus → **cakṣuṣā** (-s- becomes -ṣ- after -u-, retroflexion rule)
   - Pattern: the ending is always -ā attached to the full stem, but the stem-final -s undergoes retroflexion (becomes -ṣ-) when preceded by i or u.

2. **Nominative singular:**
   - manas → **manaḥ**
   - havis → **haviḥ**
   - cakṣus → **cakṣuḥ**
   - All three share the same pattern: the stem-final -s becomes visarga (-ḥ) at word-end. Nom/acc singular are identical in all neuter stems.

3. **Before bh- endings, the stem-final -s assimilates:**
   - manas → **manobhiḥ** (-as before bh- → -o-)
   - havis → **havirbhiḥ** (-is before bh- → -ir-)
   - cakṣus → **cakṣurbhiḥ** (-us before bh- → -ur-)

**E3.**
1. **manas — all cases singular:**
   - 1. Nom: manaḥ
   - 2. Acc: manaḥ
   - 3. Inst: manasā
   - 4. Dat: manase
   - 5. Abl: manasaḥ
   - 6. Gen: manasaḥ
   - 7. Loc: manasi
   - 8. Voc: manaḥ

2. **tapas — nom and acc plural:** tapāṃsi (both nominative and accusative plural are identical in neuter)

3. **havis — inst and loc singular:** haviṣā (instrumental), haviṣi (locative)

4. **vāc — nom sg and inst sg:** vāk (nominative, c → k at word-end), vācā (instrumental)

5. **āyus — nom and acc singular:** āyuḥ (both are identical in neuter)

**E4.**
1. **Stems:** manas (mind), vacas (speech), karman (action)
2. **Stem types:** manas = neuter -as stem; vacas = neuter -as stem; karman = neuter -an stem
3. **Case:** All three are in the **instrumental singular** (tṛtīyā ekavacana) — "by mind, by speech, by action"
4. **vacas vs. vāc:** vacas is a separate -as stem noun meaning "speech/word" (neuter), distinct from vāc (feminine root stem also meaning "speech"). Both exist in Sanskrit. vacas forms its instrumental regularly as vacasā. The phrase uses the -as stem form, not the root stem.
5. **karman** is a neuter -an stem (like ātman, brahman). Its instrumental singular is **karmaṇā** — the -n of the stem becomes -ṇ after the -r- earlier in the word (retroflexion), and the ending is -ā.

**E5.** Sample composition (describing Devī):

> sā devī sarva-diśāṃ śobhā asti.
> tasyāḥ tejasā jagat prakāśate.
> tasyāḥ vāk vedāḥ eva.
> manasā tāṃ namāmi, tapasā tāṃ prārthayāmi.

Translation: "She, the Goddess, is the beauty of all directions. By her brilliance the world shines. Her speech is the Vedas themselves. With my mind I bow to her, with austerity I pray to her."

Analysis:
- -as stems used: tejas (inst. sg. tejasā), manas (inst. sg. manasā), tapas (inst. sg. tapasā)
- Root consonant stem: vāc (nom. sg. vāk), diś (gen. pl. diśām)
- Pronouns: sā, tasyāḥ, tām

Award marks for: correct stem usage (2), accurate case forms (1), coherent meaning (1), variety of constructions (1).

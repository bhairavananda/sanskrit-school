# Stage 12 Badge: Vākyakāra

**Title:** Free Builder
**Badge:** 🏅 Vākyakāra

## Requirements

Speak for 2 minutes of spontaneous Sanskrit on any topic — no English, no pre-written script. Must use at least 3 different vibhaktis and 5 different verb forms.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Voice recording in #badge-submissions

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 13 content and grants the **Vākyakāra** role on Discord.

## Vyañjana Classification

### By Position in Varga

| Position | Prāṇa | Kaṭhina/Mṛdu | Voicing |
|----------|--------|---------------|---------|
| 1st (ka, ca, ṭa, ta, pa) | Alpaprāṇa | Kaṭhina | Aghoṣa (voiceless) |
| 2nd (kha, cha, ṭha, tha, pha) | Mahāprāṇa | Kaṭhina | Aghoṣa |
| 3rd (ga, ja, ḍa, da, ba) | Alpaprāṇa | Mṛdu | Ghoṣa (voiced) |
| 4th (gha, jha, ḍha, dha, bha) | Mahāprāṇa | Mṛdu | Ghoṣa |
| 5th (ṅa, ña, ṇa, na, ma) | Alpaprāṇa | Anunāsika | Ghoṣa |

### Special Categories

| Category | Letters | Count |
|----------|---------|-------|
| Antaḥstha | ya, ra, la, va | 4 — Mṛdu, Alpaprāṇa |
| Ūṣman | śa, ṣa, sa, ha | 4 — Kaṭhina (śa, ṣa, sa), Mṛdu (ha); all Mahāprāṇa |

**Remember:** ha is mahāprāṇa but mṛdu (commonly tested point).

## Sthāna-Prayatna Chart

### Ābhyantara Prayatna (Internal Effort)

| Prayatna | Type | Letters |
|----------|------|---------|
| Spṛṣṭa | Complete contact | All 25 sparśa consonants |

| Prayatna | Letters |
|----------|---------|
| Śvāsa (voiceless) | 1st, 2nd of each varga; śa, ṣa, sa, visarga |

## Memory Aids

**Mātrā verse:**
> ekamātrā bhaved hrasvā, dvimātrā dīrgha ucyate |
> trimātrā pluta vijñeyā, vyañjanārdhamātrākam ||

**Kaṭhina vs. Mṛdu mnemonic:** "ka" starts "kaṭhina" — 1st and 2nd letters are kaṭhina (hard). The soft (mṛdu) letters are the 3rd, 4th, 5th.

**Alpa vs. Mahā mnemonic:** Odd positions (1st, 3rd, 5th) = alpaprāṇa. Even positions (2nd, 4th) = mahāprāṇa.

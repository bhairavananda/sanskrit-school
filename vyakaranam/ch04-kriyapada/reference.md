## Puruṣa-Vacana Quick Map

| Sanskrit Term | English | Refers To |
|---------------|---------|-----------|
| prathama puruṣa | 3rd person | saḥ / tau / te (he, they two, they) |
| madhyama puruṣa | 2nd person | tvam / yuvām / yūyam (you, you two, you all) |
| uttama puruṣa | 1st person | aham / āvām / vayam (I, we two, we) |
| ekavacana | singular | one |
| dvivacana | dual | two |
| bahuvacana | plural | many |

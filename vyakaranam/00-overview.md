# Vyākaraṇam — High School Sanskrit Grammar

Video lectures by **Dr. Venkata Subramanian**, covering foundational Pāṇinian grammar in 100 sessions across 4 chapters.

[YouTube Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLmozlYyYE-ET72Pz5n8WDtKHH31K06QhW)

## Course Structure

| Ch | Topic | Lectures | Focus |
|----|-------|----------|-------|
| 0 | Introduction | 5 | Prārthana, rules, scope |
| 1 | Varṇavicāraḥ | 21 | Letters, classification, svaras, vyañjanas, varṇotpatti, Māheśvara Sūtras |
| 2 | Sandhi | 56 | 27 sandhi types with practice sessions |
| 3 | Sarvānāmāni | 3 | Pronouns |
| 4 | Kriyāpada | 15 | Dhātu, vikaraṇa, lakāra, tiṅ pratyaya, laṭ/lṛṭ conjugation |

## How to Use

Each chapter page lists every lecture with a direct YouTube link. Work through them in order — theory lectures are followed by practice sessions and periodic Q&A reviews.

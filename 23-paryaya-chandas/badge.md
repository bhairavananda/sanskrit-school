# Stage 23 Badge: Padavit

**Title:** Lexical Acrobat
**Badge:** 🏅 Padavit

## Requirements

Take a given verse and rewrite it replacing 4+ words with synonyms while maintaining the exact meter. Show both versions.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 24 content and grants the **Padavit** role on Discord.

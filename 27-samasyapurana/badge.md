# Stage 27 Badge: Samasyāpūraka

**Title:** Backwards Builder
**Badge:** 🏅 Samasyāpūraka

## Requirements

Given 3 samasyā prompts (final pāda of a verse), complete each into a full anuṣṭubh śloka.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post — prompts given by moderator

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 28 content and grants the **Samasyāpūraka** role on Discord.

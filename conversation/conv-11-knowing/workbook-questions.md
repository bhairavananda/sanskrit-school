# Stage 11: Knowing & Not Knowing — Workbook

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)
*Write in IAST.*

1. I know.
2. Do you know Sanskrit?
3. She doesn't know the way.
4. I didn't know that.
5. Who knows?

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. ahaṃ na jānāmi.
2. saḥ sarvaṃ jānāti.
3. kiṃ tvaṃ jānāsi?
4. mama na jñātam.
5. vayaṃ jānīmaḥ.

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)
*Fill in the missing word.*

1. ahaṃ ______. (I know.)
2. ahaṃ ______ jānāmi. (I don't know.)
3. kiṃ tvaṃ ______? (Do you know?)
4. mama na ______. (I didn't know that.)
5. sā mārgaṃ na ______. (She doesn't know the way.)

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)
*Write a 3-4 line dialogue in IAST for each prompt.*

1. You ask a friend if they know where the shop is. They don't know. You say you know and offer to show the way.

2. Someone tells you surprising news (an exam tomorrow, a friend is coming, etc.). You say you didn't know. They explain, and you say "now I know."

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks)

1. Write about three things you know and two things you don't know (3-4 sentences in IAST).

2. Write a short scene where someone realizes something they didn't know before, using jñātam and idānīm jñātam (3-4 sentences in IAST).

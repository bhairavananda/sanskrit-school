# Stage 8: Going & Coming — Workbook

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)
*Write in IAST.*

1. I am going to the garden.
2. Where are you going?
3. Come here quickly!
4. Let's go there (just us two).
5. She is not coming.

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. aham āpaṇaṃ gacchāmi.
2. tvam kutra gacchasi?
3. vayam udyānaṃ gacchāmaḥ.
4. śīghram āgaccha!
5. saḥ gṛhaṃ gacchati.

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)
*Fill in the missing word.*

1. aham tatra ______. (I am going there.)
2. tvam ______ gacchasi? (Where are you going?)
3. atra ______! (Come here!)
4. āvāṃ tatra ______. (Let's go there — dual.)
5. sā gṛhaṃ ______. (She is going home.)

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)
*Write a 3-4 line dialogue in IAST for each prompt.*

1. You ask a friend where they are going. They say the shop. You say "let's go together" (use dual).

2. Someone calls you to come quickly. You ask where. They say "to the garden." You agree and say you're coming.

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks)

1. Describe the movements of three different people — where each is going (3-4 sentences in IAST).

2. Write a short invitation asking someone to come to your house, using āgaccha/āgacchatu and location words (3-4 sentences in IAST).

# Stage 8: Going & Coming — Answer Key

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

1. aham udyānaṃ gacchāmi.
2. tvam kutra gacchasi?
3. atra śīghram āgaccha!
4. āvāṃ tatra gacchāvaḥ.
5. sā na āgacchati.

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. I am going to the shop.
2. Where are you going?
3. Let's go to the garden (all of us).
4. Come quickly!
5. He is going home.

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

1. aham tatra **gacchāmi**.
2. tvam **kutra** gacchasi?
3. atra **āgaccha**!
4. āvāṃ tatra **gacchāvaḥ**.
5. sā gṛhaṃ **gacchati**.

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)

### Dialogue 1 (sample)
> A: tvam kutra gacchasi?
> B: aham āpaṇaṃ gacchāmi.
> A: āvāṃ gacchāvaḥ!
> B: ām, āgaccha!

### Dialogue 2 (sample)
> A: śīghram āgaccha!
> B: kutra?
> A: udyānaṃ gacchāvaḥ.
> B: samyak, aham āgacchāmi.

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks)

### Sample 1
rāmaḥ āpaṇaṃ gacchati. sītā udyānaṃ gacchati. aham gṛhaṃ gacchāmi. sarve bhinnāṃ diśāṃ gacchanti!

### Sample 2
mama gṛham āgacchatu. tatra phālani santi, dugdham asti ca. āvāṃ tatra upaviśāvaḥ. śīghram āgaccha!

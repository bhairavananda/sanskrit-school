## Section C: Choose the Right Lakāra (10 marks)

For each sentence, choose the correct form from the options. Explain WHY that lakāra fits.

1. "The guru _____ (spoke) these words." (2 marks)
   a) avadat (Laṅ)  b) uvāca (Liṭ)  c) avocat (Luṅ)

2. "I _____ (am going to do) this work, it's decided." (2 marks)
   a) kariṣyāmi (Lṛṭ)  b) kartā asmi (Luṭ)  c) kuryām (Liṅ)

3. "Suddenly lightning _____! (struck)" (2 marks)
   a) abhavat (Laṅ)  b) babhūva (Liṭ)  c) abhūt (Luṅ)

4. "May knowledge _____ (arise)!" (2 marks)
   a) bhavatu (Loṭ)  b) bhūyāt (āśīrliṅ)  c) bhavet (Liṅ)

5. "Long ago, a sage _____ (went) to the forest." (2 marks)
   a) agacchat (Laṅ)  b) jagāma (Liṭ)  c) agamat (Luṅ)

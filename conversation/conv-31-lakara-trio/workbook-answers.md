# Stage 31: Blessings, Promises & Vivid Past — Workbook (Answers)

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit

1. **kim abhūt?** (Luṅ — aorist of bhū, punctual "what happened?")

2. **purā ekaḥ mahārājā babhūva.** (Liṭ — perfect of bhū, narrative story opening)

3. **ahaṃ śvaḥ vaktā asmi.** (Luṭ — periphrastic future of vac, definite/committed)

4. **sarveṣāṃ maṅgalaṃ bhūyāt!** (āśīrliṅ — benedictive of bhū)

5. **saḥ uvāca — "ahaṃ na āgamiṣyāmi" iti.** (Liṭ — perfect of vac for elevated quotation frame)

## Section B: Translate to English

1. She fell — everyone saw (it). (apātat = Luṅ of pat; adrākṣuḥ = Luṅ 3rd pl. of dṛś)

2. Long ago, there was a tree here. (babhūva = Liṭ of bhū, narrative past)

3. I am going to go tomorrow. (gantā asmi = Luṭ of gam, definite future)

4. May there be auspiciousness for you! (bhūyāt = āśīrliṅ of bhū)

5. The Lord spoke — "the field and the knower of the field" — thus. (uvāca = Liṭ of vac, Gītā quotation frame)

## Section C: Choose the Right Lakāra

1. **b) uvāca (Liṭ)** — A guru speaking weighty words calls for the elevated, literary perfect. Laṅ would be casual; Luṅ would imply sudden/surprised speech.

2. **b) kartā asmi (Luṭ)** — The phrase "it's decided" signals definite commitment — exactly Luṭ's domain. Lṛṭ (kariṣyāmi) is general future without the commitment. Liṅ (kuryām) is hypothetical.

3. **c) abhūt (Luṅ)** — "Suddenly" signals punctual, vivid, instantaneous — the aorist's specialty. Laṅ would narrate it as ongoing; Liṭ would be too literary.

4. **b) bhūyāt (āśīrliṅ)** — A formal blessing/wish. Loṭ (bhavatu) would also work for everyday blessing, but bhūyāt is more ritually potent. Liṅ bhavet is hypothetical, not a wish.

5. **b) jagāma (Liṭ)** — "Long ago" (purā) signals narrative/literary past — the perfect's domain. Laṅ would work in casual narration. Luṅ would be too vivid/immediate for "long ago."

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue

**D1.** Sample:
> **A:** bhoḥ! kim abhūt? tava mukhaṃ prasannam asti!
> **B:** aho! mama putraḥ parīkṣāyāṃ prathamaḥ abhūt!
> **A:** sādhu! sādhu! adbhutam!
> **B:** tava āśīrvādena. tasya maṅgalaṃ bhūyāt!

**D2.** Sample:
> **A:** purā kasmiṃścit grāme ekaḥ vṛddhaḥ babhūva.
> **B:** saḥ kiṃ cakāra?
> **A:** saḥ uvāca — "ahaṃ śvaḥ nagaraṃ gantā asmi, sarveṣāṃ kṛte dhanaṃ ānayitā asmi" iti.
> **B:** tataḥ kim abhūt?

## Section E: Free Response

**E1.** Sample blessings:
- Wedding: *yuvayoḥ dāmpatyaṃ ciraṃ bhūyāt! sukhinau bhavatam!* (May your married life endure! May you two be happy!)
- Graduation: *tava vidyā vṛddhiṃ prāpnuyāt! yaśaḥ bhūyāt!* (May your knowledge grow! May there be glory!)
- Temple: *śubham astu! sarveṣāṃ maṅgalaṃ bhūyāt! bhagavān rakṣatu!* (May there be auspiciousness! May God protect!)

**E2.** Sample:
*adya prātaḥ ahaṃ mārge agaccham. ekākasmāt meghaḥ agarjat! varṣā abhūt! ahaṃ dhāvitavān — gṛhaṃ prāptavān — kintu pūrṇaṃ klinnaḥ abhūvam! kim abhūt iti mātā apṛcchat. ahaṃ ahāsam.*
(This morning I was walking on the road. Suddenly thunder roared! Rain happened! I ran — reached home — but was completely drenched! "What happened?" mother asked. I laughed.)

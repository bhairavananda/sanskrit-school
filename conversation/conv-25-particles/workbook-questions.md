# Stage 25: Particles & Emphasis — Workbook

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

Use Devanagari and IAST. Each sentence must use the particle indicated.

1. Only Sita knows the answer. *(use eva)*
2. Even the teacher was surprised. *(use api)*
3. They say the king is ill. *(use kila)*
4. Isn't this your book? *(use nanu)*
5. Tea or coffee? *(use vā)*

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. अहम् एव तत् करोमि। (aham eva tat karomi.)
2. सः खलु विद्वान् अस्ति। (saḥ khalu vidvān asti.)
3. बालकः अपि जानाति। (bālakaḥ api jānāti.)
4. सा किल गायति। (sā kila gāyati.)
5. ननु त्वं श्रुतवान्? (nanu tvaṃ śrutavān?)

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

Choose the correct particle (eva, api, khalu, nanu, kila, tu, vā) for each blank.

1. अहं पठामि, सः ___ क्रीडति। (I study, but he plays.)
2. सः ___ सत्यं वदति। (He certainly speaks the truth.)
3. गुरुः ___ अवदत्। (The guru himself said it.)
4. दुग्धं ___ जलं ___ ? (Milk or water?)
5. सा ___ देशं त्यक्तवती। (They say she left the country.)

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (5 marks)

**Prompt 1:** Two friends are deciding what to eat. One asks "rice or bread?" The other says "even bread is fine" and adds "but the rice is certainly fresh." Write 3-4 lines.

**Prompt 2:** Someone shares a rumor (use kila). The other person responds with a rhetorical question (use nanu) doubting the rumor. Write 3-4 lines.

---

## Section E: Free Response (5 marks)

1. Write 3 sentences about yourself using three different particles (eva, api, khalu, kila, or nanu).

2. Take the sentence सः गच्छति (saḥ gacchati — "He goes") and write out five versions of it, each using a different particle, with English translations showing how the meaning shifts.

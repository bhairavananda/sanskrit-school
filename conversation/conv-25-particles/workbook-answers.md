# Stage 25: Particles & Emphasis — Answer Key

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

1. सीता एव उत्तरं जानाति। (sītā eva uttaraṃ jānāti.)
2. गुरुः अपि विस्मितः आसीत्। (guruḥ api vismitaḥ āsīt.)
3. नृपः किल रुग्णः अस्ति। (nṛpaḥ kila rugṇaḥ asti.)
4. ननु एतत् तव पुस्तकम्? (nanu etat tava pustakam?)
5. चायं वा काफी वा? (cāyaṃ vā kāphī vā?)

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. I myself do it. / Only I do it.
2. He is certainly a scholar.
3. Even a child knows. / A child also knows.
4. They say she sings. / She reportedly sings.
5. Didn't you hear? / Surely you heard?

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

1. **तु** (tu) — mild contrast: "I study, but he plays."
2. **खलु** (khalu) — certainty: "He certainly speaks the truth."
3. **एव** (eva) — emphasis: "The guru himself said it."
4. **वा ... वा** (vā ... vā) — "Milk or water?"
5. **किल** (kila) — hearsay: "They say she left the country."

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (5 marks)

**Prompt 1 — Sample:**

> **क:** ओदनं वा रोटिका वा? (odanaṃ vā roṭikā vā?)
> "Rice or bread?"
>
> **ख:** रोटिका अपि शोभना। (roṭikā api śobhanā.)
> "Even bread is fine."
>
> **क:** किन्तु ओदनं खलु नूतनम्। (kintu odanaṃ khalu nūtanam.)
> "But the rice is certainly fresh."
>
> **ख:** तर्हि ओदनम् एव ददातु। (tarhi odanam eva dadātu.)
> "Then give only rice."

**Prompt 2 — Sample:**

> **क:** नृपः किल सिंहासनं त्यक्तवान्। (nṛpaḥ kila siṃhāsanaṃ tyaktavān.)
> "They say the king abandoned the throne."
>
> **ख:** ननु सः धीरः आसीत्? (nanu saḥ dhīraḥ āsīt?)
> "Wasn't he a brave man?"
>
> **क:** सत्यम् एव। किन्तु जनाः तु एवं वदन्ति। (satyam eva. kintu janāḥ tu evaṃ vadanti.)
> "True indeed. But people say so."

## Section E: Free Response — Sample Answers (5 marks)

**1. Three sentences with different particles:**

- अहम् एव मम भोजनं पचामि। (aham eva mama bhojanaṃ pacāmi.) — "I myself cook my food."
- अहं कदाचित् संस्कृतम् अपि वदामि। (ahaṃ kadācit saṃskṛtam api vadāmi.) — "I sometimes speak Sanskrit too."
- ननु एतत् सुन्दरम्? (nanu etat sundaram?) — "Isn't this beautiful?"

**2. Five versions of सः गच्छति:**

- सः एव गच्छति (saḥ eva gacchati) — "Only HE goes." / "He himself goes."
- सः अपि गच्छति (saḥ api gacchati) — "He ALSO goes." / "Even he goes."
- सः खलु गच्छति (saḥ khalu gacchati) — "He certainly goes."
- सः किल गच्छति (saḥ kila gacchati) — "They say he goes." / "He reportedly goes."
- ननु सः गच्छति? (nanu saḥ gacchati?) — "Doesn't he go?" / "Surely he goes?"

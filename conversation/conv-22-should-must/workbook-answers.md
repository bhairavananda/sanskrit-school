# Stage 22: Should & Must — Workbook Answers

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit

1. **त्वया एतत् पुस्तकं पठनीयम्।**
   *tvayā etat pustakaṃ paṭhanīyam.*

2. **मया अद्य विद्यालयं गन्तव्यम्।**
   *mayā adya vidyālayaṃ gantavyam.*

3. **मा तत् भोजनं खाद।**
   *mā tat bhojanaṃ khāda.*

4. **सा प्रथमं वदतु।**
   *sā prathamaṃ vadatu.*

5. **सर्वैः परस्परं साहाय्यं कर्तव्यम्।**
   *sarvaiḥ parasparaṃ sāhāyyaṃ kartavyam.*

---

## Section B: Translate to English

1. You should exercise every day.

2. Don't go there. That place is dangerous.

3. Children should drink milk.

4. Let him stay. I will go.

5. I must complete this work tomorrow.

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank

1. **त्वया** (*tvayā*) — by you (instrumental of tvam)

2. **मा** (*mā*) — the prohibitive particle

3. **गायतु** (*gāyatu*) — let her sing (third-person imperative of gai)

4. **वक्तव्यम्** (*vaktavyam*) — should be spoken (gerundive of vac)

5. **सेवनीयम्** (*sevanīyam*) — must be taken/consumed (gerundive of sev)

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue

**1. Bedtime routine:**

-- पुत्र, शयनात् पूर्वं त्वया पाठः पठनीयः।
   *putra, śayanāt pūrvaṃ tvayā pāṭhaḥ paṭhanīyaḥ.*
   Son, before sleeping you should study the lesson.

-- किम् अन्यत् कर्तव्यम्?
   *kim anyat kartavyam?*
   What else should I do?

-- दुग्धं पातव्यम्। मा क्रीडां कुरु। अधुना विश्रामस्य समयः।
   *dugdhaṃ pātavyam. mā krīḍāṃ kuru. adhunā viśrāmasya samayaḥ.*
   You should drink milk. Don't play. Now it's time for rest.

**2. Party planning:**

-- उत्सवाय कः किं करोतु? रामः भोजनं पचतु।
   *utsavāya kaḥ kiṃ karotu? rāmaḥ bhojanaṃ pacatu.*
   For the party, who should do what? Let Rama cook the food.

-- मया पुष्पाणि क्रेतव्यानि। सीता गृहम् अलङ्करोतु।
   *mayā puṣpāṇi kretavyāni. sītā gṛham alaṅkarotu.*
   I must buy flowers. Let Sita decorate the house.

-- आम्, सर्वैः मिलित्वा कार्यं कर्तव्यम्। उत्सवः शोभनः भविष्यति!
   *ām, sarvaiḥ militvā kāryaṃ kartavyam. utsavaḥ śobhanaḥ bhaviṣyati!*
   Yes, everyone should work together. The party will be wonderful!

## Section E: Free Response (Sample Answers)

**1. Classroom rules:**
कक्षायां सर्वैः शान्तिः पालनीया। छात्रैः ध्यानेन श्रोतव्यम्। दूरवाणी न उपयोक्तव्या। सर्वैः गुरुः सम्मन्तव्यः।
*kakṣāyāṃ sarvaiḥ śāntiḥ pālanīyā. chātraiḥ dhyānena śrotavyam. dūravāṇī na upayoktavyā. sarvaiḥ guruḥ sammantavyaḥ.*
In the classroom, silence should be maintained by all. Students should listen attentively. Phones should not be used. The teacher should be respected by all.

**2. Exam advice:**
मित्र, मा चिन्तां कुरु। त्वया केवलं शान्त्या पठनीयम्। नूनं त्वं उत्तीर्णः भविष्यसि। त्वया रात्रौ विश्रामः अपि कर्तव्यः।
*mitra, mā cintāṃ kuru. tvayā kevalaṃ śāntyā paṭhanīyam. nūnaṃ tvaṃ uttīrṇaḥ bhaviṣyasi. tvayā rātrau viśrāmaḥ api kartavyaḥ.*
Friend, don't worry. You should just study calmly. Surely you will pass. You should also rest at night.

# Stage 22: Should & Must — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks, 2 each)

1. You should read this book.
2. I must go to school today.
3. Don't eat that food.
4. Let her speak first.
5. Everyone should help each other.

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks, 2 each)

1. त्वया प्रतिदिनं व्यायामः कर्तव्यः।
   *tvayā pratidinaṃ vyāyāmaḥ kartavyaḥ.*

2. मा तत्र गच्छ। तत् स्थानं भयङ्करम् अस्ति।
   *mā tatra gaccha. tat sthānaṃ bhayaṅkaram asti.*

3. बालकैः दुग्धं पातव्यम्।
   *bālakaiḥ dugdhaṃ pātavyam.*

4. सः तिष्ठतु। अहं गच्छामि।
   *saḥ tiṣṭhatu. ahaṃ gacchāmi.*

5. मया एतत् कार्यं श्वः पूर्णीकरणीयम्।
   *mayā etat kāryaṃ śvaḥ pūrṇīkaraṇīyam.*

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks, 2 each)

1. __________ अद्य गृहकार्यं कर्तव्यम्। (by you — supply the instrumental)
   *__________ adya gṛhakāryaṃ kartavyam.*

2. __________ कोपं कुरु। (Don't — supply the prohibitive particle)
   *__________ kopaṃ kuru.*

3. सा अत्र __________। वयं श्रुणुमः। (let her sing — supply third-person imperative of gai)
   *sā atra __________. vayaṃ śṛṇumaḥ.*

4. सर्वैः सत्यं __________। (should be spoken — supply the gerundive of vac)
   *sarvaiḥ satyaṃ __________.*

5. बालकेन औषधं __________। (must be taken — supply the gerundive of sev)
   *bālakena auṣadhaṃ __________.*

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks, 3 each)

1. **Prompt:** A parent tells a child what they should do before going to bed (study, drink milk, don't play). Write 3-4 lines using *kartavyam* and *mā kuru*.

2. **Prompt:** Friends are dividing tasks for a party. One says "let him cook," another says "I must buy flowers," etc. Write 3-4 lines using *-tu* (imperative) and *mayā...kartavyam*.

---

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks, 2 each)

1. Write 2-3 rules for a classroom using gerundives (*kartavyam / na kartavyam*). For example: "Everyone should listen" or "Phones should not be used."

2. Give advice to a friend who is nervous before an exam. Use *mā cintāṃ kuru*, *tvayā...kartavyam*, and *nūnam* (from Stage 21). Write 2-3 sentences.

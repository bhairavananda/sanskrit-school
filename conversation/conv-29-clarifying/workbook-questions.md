# Stage 29: Clarifying & Repairing — Workbook

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

1. Sorry, I didn't understand.
2. What is the meaning of the word 'guru'?
3. How do you say 'water' in Sanskrit?
4. Please speak slowly.
5. Can you repeat that?

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. punaḥ vadatu, na avagatam — पुनः वदतु, न अवगतम्
2. 'saṃskṛtam' iti padasya ko'rthaḥ? — 'संस्कृतम्' इति पदस्य कोऽर्थः?
3. arthāt sā adya na āgamiṣyati — अर्थात् सा अद्य न आगमिष्यति
4. kṛpayā spaṣṭaṃ vadatu, na avagacchāmi — कृपया स्पष्टं वदतु, न अवगच्छामि
5. idānīm avagatam, dhanyavādaḥ — इदानीम् अवगतम्, धन्यवादः

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

1. kṣamyatām, ______ vadatu.
   *(Sorry, say that again.)*

2. na ______. kṛpayā śanaiḥ vadatu.
   *(I didn't understand. Please speak slowly.)*

3. 'tree' iti saṃskṛte ______ ucyate?
   *(How do you say 'tree' in Sanskrit?)*

4. 'mokṣaḥ' iti padasya ______?
   *(What is the meaning of the word 'mokṣa'?)*

5. ______ saḥ parīkṣāyāṃ na utīrṇaḥ.
   *(That is to say, he did not pass the exam.)*

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)

**D1.** Write a 3-4 line dialogue where a Sanskrit learner asks their teacher the meaning of an unfamiliar word. The teacher explains, and the student confirms understanding. Use at least two clarifying phrases from this stage.

**D2.** Write a 3-4 line dialogue where someone is speaking too fast and the listener asks them to slow down and repeat. Use *śanaiḥ vadatu*, *punaḥ vadatu*, and *avagatam*.

---

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks)

**E1.** You're in a Sanskrit conversation class and you hear a word you don't know. Write 2-3 sentences asking about the word, getting an answer, and confirming you understood.

**E2.** Write 2-3 sentences where you ask how to say three different English words in Sanskrit, using the *katham ucyate* pattern.

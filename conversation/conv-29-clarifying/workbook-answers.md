# Stage 29: Clarifying & Repairing — Answer Key

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

1. kṣamyatām, na avagatam.
   क्षम्यताम्, न अवगतम्।

2. 'guru'-śabdasya ko'rthaḥ?
   'गुरु'-शब्दस्य कोऽर्थः?

3. 'water' iti saṃskṛte katham ucyate?
   'water' इति संस्कृते कथम् उच्यते?

4. kṛpayā śanaiḥ vadatu.
   कृपया शनैः वदतु।

5. kiṃ punaḥ vaktuṃ śaknoṣi?
   किं पुनः वक्तुं शक्नोषि?

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. Say that again, I didn't understand.
2. What is the meaning of the word 'saṃskṛtam'?
3. That is to say, she won't come today.
4. Please speak clearly, I don't understand.
5. Now I understood, thank you.

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

1. **punaḥ** — kṣamyatām, **punaḥ** vadatu.
2. **avagatam** — na **avagatam**. kṛpayā śanaiḥ vadatu.
3. **katham** — 'tree' iti saṃskṛte **katham** ucyate?
4. **ko'rthaḥ** — 'mokṣaḥ' iti padasya **ko'rthaḥ**?
5. **arthāt** — **arthāt** saḥ parīkṣāyāṃ na utīrṇaḥ.

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)

**D1.** Sample:

**chātraḥ:** guro, 'prajñā' iti padasya ko'rthaḥ?
**guruḥ:** prajñā arthāt 'viśeṣa-jñānam' iti. āṅgle 'wisdom' iti ucyate.
**chātraḥ:** idānīm avagatam. prajñā arthāt wisdom. dhanyavādaḥ!

*(Student: Teacher, what does the word 'prajñā' mean? / Teacher: Prajñā, that is to say, 'special knowledge.' In English it's called 'wisdom.' / Student: Now I understand. Prajñā means wisdom. Thank you!)*

**D2.** Sample:

**anilā:** hyaḥ mama bhaginī āgatavatī vayaṃ nagaraṃ gatvā kīḍitavatyaḥ...
**meghaḥ:** śanaiḥ vadatu! bahu śīghraṃ vadasi. punaḥ vadatu kṛpayā.
**anilā:** hyaḥ... mama bhaginī... āgatavatī. avagatam?
**meghaḥ:** ām, avagatam! tataḥ kim abhavat?

*(Anila: Yesterday my sister came and we went to the city and played... / Megha: Speak slowly! You speak too fast. Please say again. / Anila: Yesterday... my sister... came. Understood? / Megha: Yes, understood! Then what happened?)*

## Section E: Free Response — Sample Answers (4 marks)

**E1.** "ācārya, 'viveka'-śabdasya ko'rthaḥ? na avagacchāmi etat padam." ācāryaḥ avadat yat viveka-śabdasya arthaḥ 'discrimination' iti. idānīm avagatam — viveka arthāt samyak-nirṇayaḥ.

*(Teacher, what does the word 'viveka' mean? I don't understand this word. The teacher said that the meaning of viveka is 'discrimination.' Now I understand — viveka means proper discernment.)*

**E2.** 'computer' iti saṃskṛte katham ucyate? saṅgaṇakaḥ iti ucyate. 'telephone' iti saṃskṛte katham ucyate? dūravāṇī iti ucyate. 'train' iti saṃskṛte katham ucyate? lohapathagāminī iti ucyate.

*(How do you say 'computer' in Sanskrit? It's called saṅgaṇaka. How do you say 'telephone' in Sanskrit? It's called dūravāṇī. How do you say 'train' in Sanskrit? It's called lohapathagāminī.)*

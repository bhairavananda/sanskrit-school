# Stage 17: When & While — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks, 2 each)

Use yadā...tadā, -samaye, yāvat...tāvat, or yatra...tatra as appropriate.

1. When the bell rings, then the class begins.
2. At dinnertime, the family sits together.
3. While the children play, the mother cooks.
4. Where there are trees, there is shade.
5. Whenever I hear music, I feel happy.

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks, 2 each)

1. yadā rātriḥ bhavati tadā tārakāḥ dṛśyante.
2. prātaḥ-samaye vāyuḥ śītalaḥ bhavati.
3. yāvat guruḥ kathayati tāvat śiṣyāḥ śṛṇvanti.
4. yatra dhūmaḥ tatra agniḥ.
5. yadā yadā sā hasati tadā tadā sarve hasanti.

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks, 2 each)

1. _______ vasantaḥ āgacchati _______ puṣpāṇi vikasanti. (When spring comes, then flowers bloom.)
2. sandhyā-_______ dīpaṃ ज्वālayāmi. (At twilight, I light a lamp.)
3. _______ ahaṃ gṛhe asmi _______ tvaṃ āpaṇaṃ gaccha. (While I am at home, you go to the shop.)
4. _______ jalaṃ _______ jīvanam. (Where there is water, there is life.)
5. _______ saḥ na āgacchati _______ vayaṃ pratīkṣāmahe. (Until he comes, we wait.)

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (5 marks, 2.5 each)

Write a 3-4 line dialogue in Sanskrit (IAST is fine) for each situation.

1. **Situation:** Two friends discuss their morning routines. Use yadā...tadā and -samaye to describe what they do at different times.

2. **Situation:** Someone describes their favourite place — what is found where, and what they do while they are there. Use yatra...tatra and yāvat...tāvat.

---

## Section E: Free Response (5 marks, 2.5 each)

1. Describe your typical evening in 3-4 Sanskrit sentences, using at least two different temporal constructions from this stage.

2. The subhāṣita says *yatra vidyā tatra vinayaḥ*. Write your own "yatra...tatra" proverb and explain its meaning in 2-3 sentences — English or Sanskrit.

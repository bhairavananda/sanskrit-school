# Stage 19: Comparing — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks, 2 each)

1. This fruit is sweeter than that fruit.
2. She is taller than her brother.
3. Among all rivers, the Ganga is the best.
4. Your garden is like a forest.
5. This village is more peaceful than the city.

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks, 2 each)

1. संस्कृतं हिन्दीतः कठिनतरम् अस्ति।
   *saṃskṛtaṃ hindītaḥ kaṭhinataram asti.*

2. सः सिंहः इव बलवान् अस्ति।
   *saḥ siṃhaḥ iva balavān asti.*

3. सर्वेषु ऋतुषु वसन्तः सर्वोत्तमः अस्ति।
   *sarveṣu ṛtuṣu vasantaḥ sarvottamaḥ asti.*

4. तव स्वरः वीणासदृशः अस्ति।
   *tava svaraḥ vīṇāsadṛśaḥ asti.*

5. अद्य श्वस्तात् उष्णतरम् अस्ति।
   *adya śvastāt uṣṇataram asti.*

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks, 2 each)

1. एतत् पुस्तकं तस्मात् पुस्तकात् __________ अस्ति। (interesting → use -tara)
   *etat pustakaṃ tasmāt pustakāt __________ asti.*

2. सा हंसः __________ चलति। (like a swan)
   *sā haṃsaḥ __________ calati.*

3. सर्वेषु वृक्षेषु वटवृक्षः __________ अस्ति। (the greatest)
   *sarveṣu vṛkṣeṣu vaṭavṛkṣaḥ __________ asti.*

4. मम गृहं तव गृह__________ लघुतरम् अस्ति। (than — supply the ablative ending)
   *mama gṛhaṃ tava gṛha__________ laghutaram asti.*

5. एषा नदी समुद्र__________ अस्ति। (similar to the ocean)
   *eṣā nadī samudra__________ asti.*

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks, 3 each)

1. **Prompt:** Two friends are deciding which movie to watch. One says the comedy is funnier, the other says the action film is the best of all. Write 3-4 lines.

2. **Prompt:** A teacher asks a student to compare two seasons. The student says summer is hotter than winter but the rainy season is like magic. Write 3-4 lines.

---

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks, 2 each)

1. Compare your favorite food to another food. Use at least one ablative comparison (-āt + adjective-tara) and one superlative (sarvottama or -tama). Write 2-3 sentences in Sanskrit.

2. Describe someone you admire using *iva* (like) and *sadṛśa* (similar to). Write 2-3 sentences in Sanskrit.

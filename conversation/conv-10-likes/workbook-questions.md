# Stage 10: Likes & Preferences — Workbook

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)
*Write in IAST.*

1. I like music.
2. What do you like?
3. She doesn't like milk.
4. Flowers are dear to him.
5. I like tea very much.

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. mama phalaṃ rocate.
2. tava kiṃ rocate?
3. tasya krīḍā rocate.
4. mama cāyaḥ priyaḥ.
5. mama etat bhojanaṃ na rocate.

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)
*Fill in the missing word.*

1. mama saṃgītaṃ ______. (I like music.)
2. tava kiṃ ______? (What do you like?)
3. mama dugdhaṃ ______ rocate. (I don't like milk.)
4. tasyāḥ puṣpāṇi ______. (Flowers are dear to her.)
5. mama cāyaḥ ______ rocate. (I like tea very much.)

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)
*Write a 3-4 line dialogue in IAST for each prompt.*

1. Two friends discuss food preferences — one likes sweets, the other doesn't. Use rocate and na rocate.

2. Someone asks what you like. You name two things you like and one you don't. Use both rocate and priyam.

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks)

1. List four things you like and two you don't, using rocate and/or priyam (4-5 sentences in IAST).

2. Describe the preferences of two different people — what each likes and dislikes (3-4 sentences in IAST).

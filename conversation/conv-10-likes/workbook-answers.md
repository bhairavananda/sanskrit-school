# Stage 10: Likes & Preferences — Answer Key

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

1. mama saṃgītaṃ rocate.
2. tava kiṃ rocate?
3. tasyāḥ dugdhaṃ na rocate.
4. tasya puṣpāṇi priyāṇi.
5. mama cāyaḥ atyantaṃ rocate.

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. I like fruit.
2. What do you like?
3. He likes sports/games.
4. Tea is dear to me. / I like tea.
5. I don't like this food.

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

1. mama saṃgītaṃ **rocate**.
2. tava kiṃ **rocate**?
3. mama dugdhaṃ **na** rocate.
4. tasyāḥ puṣpāṇi **priyāṇi**.
5. mama cāyaḥ **atyantaṃ** rocate.

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)

### Dialogue 1 (sample)
> A: tava miṣṭānnaṃ rocate vā?
> B: ām, mama miṣṭānnaṃ atyantaṃ rocate! tava?
> A: mama miṣṭānnaṃ na rocate. mama phalāni rocante.
> B: phalāni api uttamāni!

### Dialogue 2 (sample)
> A: tava kiṃ rocate?
> B: mama saṃgītaṃ rocate, puṣpāṇi ca priyāṇi. mama dugdhaṃ na rocate.
> A: mama api saṃgītaṃ priyam!

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks)

### Sample 1
mama saṃgītaṃ rocate. mama phalāni priyāṇi. mama pustakāni rocante. mama krīḍā rocate. mama dugdhaṃ na rocate. mama nagarasya kalaśoraḥ na rocate.

### Sample 2
rāmasya krīḍā rocate. tasya phalāni priyāṇi. sītāyāḥ saṃgītaṃ rocate. tasyāḥ puṣpāṇi priyāṇi. rāmasya saṃgītaṃ na rocate. sītāyāḥ krīḍā na rocate.

# Stage 28: Reported Speech — Answer Key

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

1. "ahaṃ prasannā asmi" iti sā avadat.
   "अहं प्रसन्ना अस्मि" इति सा अवदत्।

2. saḥ avadat yat saḥ śvaḥ āgamiṣyati.
   सः अवदत् यत् सः श्वः आगमिष्यति।

3. śrutaṃ mayā yat āpaṇaḥ pihitaḥ asti.
   श्रुतं मया यत् आपणः पिहितः अस्ति।

4. "kiṃ tvam pustakam apaṭhaḥ?" iti ācāryaḥ apṛcchat.
   "किं त्वम् पुस्तकम् अपठः?" इति आचार्यः अपृच्छत्।

5. tasyāḥ mate etat bhojanaṃ śreṣṭham asti.
   तस्याः मते एतत् भोजनं श्रेष्ठम् अस्ति।

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. She said, "I don't know."
2. They say that the city is beautiful.
3. I heard that he has gone.
4. The teacher asked whether the students are ready.
5. He said "I'll come tomorrow" but didn't come.

1. **iti** — "ahaṃ kṣudhitaḥ asmi" **iti** bālaḥ avadat.
2. **yat** — sā avadat **yat** sā śvaḥ āgamiṣyati.
3. **śrutam** — **śrutam** mayā yat vṛṣṭiḥ āgamiṣyati.
4. **mate** — tasya **mate** etat kāryaṃ sugamam asti.
5. **apṛcchat** — saḥ **apṛcchat** kiṃ tvaṃ saṃskṛtaṃ vadasi.

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)

**D1.** Sample:

**rāmaḥ:** adya kiṃ bhojanaṃ bhavati?
**sītā:** mama mātā avadat yat adya bāhyabhojanaṃ kariṣyāmaḥ.
**rāmaḥ:** sā kim avadat kutra gamiṣyāmaḥ iti?
**sītā:** "nūtane bhojanālaye gamiṣyāmaḥ" iti sā avadat.

*(Ram: What's for dinner today? / Sita: My mother said that we'll eat out today. / Ram: Did she say where we'll go? / Sita: She said, "We'll go to the new restaurant.")*

**D2.** Sample:

**anilaḥ:** guruḥ avadat yat parīkṣā śukriyavāsare bhavati.
**priyā:** kintu adya guruḥ avadat yat parīkṣā somiravāsare bhavati!
**anilaḥ:** saḥ avadat yat śukriyavāsare bhavati kintu idānīṃ somiravāsare iti? kaṣṭam!
**priyā:** āṃ, idānīṃ adhikaṃ paṭhanīyam!

*(Anil: The teacher said the exam is on Friday. / Priya: But today the teacher said the exam is on Monday! / Anil: He said Friday but now Monday? Oh no! / Priya: Yes, now we need to study more!)*

## Section E: Free Response — Sample Answers (4 marks)

**E1.** śrutaṃ mayā yat nūtanaḥ saṅgaṇakaḥ āgataḥ. mama mitram avadat yat saḥ atīva śīghraḥ asti. "sarve janāḥ taṃ kretum icchanti" iti saḥ avadat.

*(I heard that a new computer has arrived. My friend said that it is very fast. He said, "Everyone wants to buy it.")*

**E2.** mama mitraṃ "ahaṃ pustakaṃ pratyarpayiṣyāmi" iti avadat. saḥ avadat yat ekasminn saptāhe pratyarpayiṣyati. kintu dvau māsau atītau, pustakaṃ na pratyāgataṃ!

*(My friend said, "I will return the book." He said that he would return it in one week. But two months have passed, and the book hasn't come back!)*

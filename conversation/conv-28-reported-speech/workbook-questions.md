# Stage 28: Reported Speech — Workbook

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

Use either the *iti* (direct) or *yat* (indirect) pattern as indicated.

1. She said, "I am happy." *(use iti)*
2. He said that he will come tomorrow. *(use yat)*
3. I heard that the shop is closed. *(use yat)*
4. The teacher asked, "Did you read the book?" *(use iti)*
5. In her opinion, this food is excellent. *(use tasyāḥ mate)*

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. "ahaṃ na jānāmi" iti sā avadат — "अहं न जानामि" इति सा अवदत्
2. te vadanti yat nagaraṃ sundaraṃ asti — ते वदन्ति यत् नगरं सुन्दरं अस्ति
3. śrutaṃ mayā yat saḥ gatvān — श्रुतं मया यत् सः गतवान्
4. guruḥ apṛcchat kiṃ chātrāḥ siddhaḥ — गुरुः अपृच्छत् किं छात्राः सिद्धाः
5. "śvaḥ āgacchāmi" iti saḥ avadat kintu na āgacchat — "श्वः आगच्छामि" इति सः अवदत् किन्तु न आगच्छत्

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

1. "ahaṃ kṣudhitaḥ asmi" ______ bālaḥ avadat.
   *(The boy said, "I am hungry." — fill the quotation marker)*

2. sā avadat ______ sā śvaḥ āgamiṣyati.
   *(She said that she would come tomorrow. — fill the complementizer)*

3. ______ mayā yat vṛṣṭiḥ āgamiṣyati.
   *(I heard that rain will come. — fill the verbal phrase)*

4. tasya ______ etat kāryaṃ sugamam asti.
   *(In his opinion, this work is easy. — fill the word for "opinion")*

5. saḥ ______ kiṃ tvaṃ saṃskṛtaṃ vadasi.
   *(He asked whether you speak Sanskrit. — fill the verb of asking)*

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)

**D1.** Write a 3-4 line dialogue where one friend tells another what their mother said about dinner plans. Use at least one *iti* construction and one *yat* construction.

**D2.** Write a 3-4 line dialogue where a student reports to a classmate what the teacher said about the exam, but the information turns out to be wrong. Use the pattern "saḥ avadat yat... kintu..."

---

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks)

**E1.** Report a piece of news you heard recently using *śrutaṃ mayā yat...* Write 2-3 sentences expanding on it.

**E2.** Write 2-3 sentences contrasting what someone promised versus what actually happened, using both *iti* and *yat* patterns.

# Stage 27: Always / Sometimes / Never — Answer Key

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

1. सा सदा सत्यं वदति। (sā sadā satyaṃ vadati.)
2. अहं कदाचित् रात्रौ पठामि। (ahaṃ kadācit rātrau paṭhāmi.)
3. सः न कदापि मिष्टान्नं खादति। (saḥ na kadāpi miṣṭānnaṃ khādati.)
4. वयं प्रत्यहम् अभ्यासं कुर्मः। (vayaṃ pratyaham abhyāsaṃ kurmaḥ.)
5. एकदा अहं वने व्याघ्रम् अपश्यम्। (ekadā ahaṃ vane vyāghram apaśyam.)

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. He usually arrives late.
2. They ask the same question again and again.
3. I rarely eat sweets.
4. She never gets angry.
5. Once a sage lived here.

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

1. **प्रत्यहम्** (pratyaham) — I study Sanskrit every day.
2. **कदाचित् ... कदाचित्** (kadācit ... kadācit) — She sometimes laughs, sometimes cries.
3. **न कदापि** (na kadāpi) — My grandfather never spoke a lie.
4. **एकदा** (ekadā) — Once there was a king.
5. **पुनः पुनः** (punaḥ punaḥ) — The boy plays that game again and again.

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (5 marks)

**Prompt 1 — Sample:**

> **माता** — त्वं प्रत्यहं दन्तान् मार्जयसि किम्? (tvaṃ pratyahaṃ dantān mārjayasi kim?)
> "Do you brush your teeth every day?"
>
> **बालकः** — आम्, सदा मार्जयामि। किन्तु कदाचित् जलपानं विस्मरामि। (ām, sadā mārjayāmi. kintu kadācit jalapānaṃ vismarāmi.)
> "Yes, I always brush. But I sometimes forget to drink water."
>
> **माता** — जलं न कदापि विस्मर! शरीरस्य कृते आवश्यकम्। (jalaṃ na kadāpi vismara! śarīrasya kṛte āvaśyakam.)
> "Never forget water! It's necessary for the body."
>
> **बालकः** — आम् माते, प्रत्यहं पास्यामि। (ām māte, pratyahaṃ pāsyāmi.)
> "Yes mother, I will drink every day."

**Prompt 2 — Sample:**

> **क:** अहं प्रत्यहं मन्दिरं गच्छामि। त्वम्? (ahaṃ pratyahaṃ mandiraṃ gacchāmi. tvam?)
> "I go to the temple every day. You?"
>
> **ख:** अहं विरलं मन्दिरं गच्छामि। (ahaṃ viralaṃ mandiraṃ gacchāmi.)
> "I rarely go to the temple."
>
> **क:** तर्हि प्रार्थनां कदा करोषि? (tarhi prārthanāṃ kadā karoṣi?)
> "Then when do you pray?"
>
> **ख:** अहं प्रायः गृहे एव प्रार्थयामि। (ahaṃ prāyaḥ gṛhe eva prārthayāmi.)
> "I usually pray at home itself."

## Section E: Free Response — Sample Answers (5 marks)

**1. Three habits:**

- अहं प्रत्यहं प्रातः चायं पिबामि। (ahaṃ pratyahaṃ prātaḥ cāyaṃ pibāmi.) — "I drink tea every morning."
- अहं कदाचित् रात्रौ भ्रमणाय गच्छामि। (ahaṃ kadācit rātrau bhramaṇāya gacchāmi.) — "I sometimes go for a walk at night."
- अहं न कदापि मद्यं पिबामि। (ahaṃ na kadāpi madyaṃ pibāmi.) — "I never drink alcohol."

**2. An anecdote with ekadā:**

- एकदा अहं बाल्ये वने भ्रान्तः। (ekadā ahaṃ bālye vane bhrāntaḥ.) — "Once in my childhood I got lost in a forest."
- अहं पुनः पुनः मातरम् अवदम् किन्तु कोऽपि न अशृणोत्। (ahaṃ punaḥ punaḥ mātaram avadam kintu ko'pi na aśṛṇot.) — "I called out for mother again and again but no one heard."
- ततः एकः वृद्धः मां गृहं प्रापयत्। (tataḥ ekaḥ vṛddhaḥ māṃ gṛhaṃ prāpayat.) — "Then an old man brought me home."

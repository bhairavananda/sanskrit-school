# Stage 27: Always / Sometimes / Never — Workbook

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

Use Devanagari and IAST.

1. She always speaks the truth.
2. I sometimes read at night.
3. He never eats sweets.
4. We practice every day.
5. Once I saw a tiger in the forest.

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. सः प्रायः विलम्बेन आगच्छति। (saḥ prāyaḥ vilambena āgacchati.)
2. ते पुनः पुनः तम् एव प्रश्नं पृच्छन्ति। (te punaḥ punaḥ tam eva praśnaṃ pṛcchanti.)
3. अहं विरलं मधुरं खादामि। (ahaṃ viralaṃ madhuraṃ khādāmi.)
4. सा न कदापि क्रुध्यति। (sā na kadāpi krudhyati.)
5. एकदा अत्र एकः मुनिः अवसत्। (ekadā atra ekaḥ muniḥ avasat.)

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

Choose the correct frequency word for each blank.

1. अहं ___ संस्कृतं पठामि। (I study Sanskrit every day.) *(pratyaham / kadācit)*
2. सा ___ हसति, ___ रोदिति। (She sometimes laughs, sometimes cries.) *(sadā / kadācit)*
3. मम पितामहः ___ असत्यं न अवदत्। (My grandfather never spoke a lie.) *(viralam / na kadāpi)*
4. ___ एकः राजा आसीत्। (Once there was a king.) *(ekadā / sadā)*
5. बालकः ___ तां क्रीडां क्रीडति। (The boy plays that game again and again.) *(prāyaḥ / punaḥ punaḥ)*

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (5 marks)

**Prompt 1:** A parent asks a child about their habits. The child says they always brush their teeth but sometimes forget to drink water. The parent says "never forget water!" Write 3-4 lines.

**Prompt 2:** Two friends compare how often they visit a temple. One says "every day," the other says "rarely — but I usually pray at home." Write 3-4 lines.

---

## Section E: Free Response (5 marks)

1. Describe three habits of yours using three different frequency words (e.g., sadā, kadācit, na kadāpi, pratyaham, prāyaḥ). Write in Sanskrit with IAST and English.

2. Tell a short anecdote starting with ekadā (once). Write 2-3 sentences in Sanskrit describing something that happened to you.

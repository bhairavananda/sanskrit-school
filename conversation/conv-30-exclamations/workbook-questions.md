## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

1. Amazing! You did this yourself!
2. Alas! She is very ill.
3. Hey Sita! Come here quickly!
4. Impossible! I don't believe this.
5. Well done! Your Sanskrit is excellent.

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. aho! etat nagaram atīva sundaram — अहो! एतत् नगरम् अतीव सुन्दरम्
2. kaṣṭam! vṛṣṭiḥ āgatā, vayaṃ na gantum śaknumaḥ — कष्टम्! वृष्टिः आगता, वयं न गन्तुं शक्नुमः
3. dhik tvām! tvaṃ vacanaṃ na pālitavān — धिक् त्वाम्! त्वं वचनं न पालितवान्
4. sādhu uktam! tava vicāraḥ samyak asti — साधु उक्तम्! तव विचारः सम्यक् अस्ति
5. ciraṃ jīva, putrī! tava jayaḥ bhavatu — चिरं जीव, पुत्री! तव जयः भवतु

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

1. ______! tvaṃ parīkṣāyām prathamaḥ āsīḥ!
   *(Bravo! You were first in the exam!)*

2. ______ tvām! tvaṃ tasya dhanaṃ coritavān!
   *(Shame on you! You stole his money!)*

3. hā ______! mama priyaḥ śvā mṛtaḥ.
   *(Alas! My dear dog has died.)*

4. ______ rāma! atra āgaccha, tvaṃ etat paśya!
   *(Hey Ram! Come here, look at this!)*

5. ______! etat kartuṃ kasyāpi na śakyam!
   *(Impossible! No one can do this!)*

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)

**D1.** Write a 3-4 line dialogue where one person shares good news (a promotion, a birth, a victory) and the other reacts with appropriate exclamations. Use at least three different exclamations from this stage.

**D2.** Write a 3-4 line dialogue where one person shares shocking or bad news and the other reacts with dismay, disbelief, and sympathy. Use at least three different exclamations.

---

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks)

**E1.** Describe a scene where someone achieves something great. Write 3-4 sentences using at least three exclamations naturally woven into the narration.

**E2.** Write 3-4 sentences reacting to surprising or disappointing news, using a variety of exclamations and showing emotional range.

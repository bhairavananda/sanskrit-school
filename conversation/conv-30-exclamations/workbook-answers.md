# Stage 30: Exclamations & Reactions — Answer Key

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

1. adbhutam! tvam etat svayam akāraḥ!
   अद्भुतम्! त्वम् एतत् स्वयम् अकारः!

2. hā hanta! sā atīva rugṇā asti.
   हा हन्त! सा अतीव रुग्णा अस्ति।

3. bhoḥ sīte! atra śīghram āgaccha!
   भोः सीते! अत्र शीघ्रम् आगच्छ!

4. asambhavam! ahaṃ na etat viśvasimi.
   असम्भवम्! अहं न एतत् विश्वसिमि।

5. sādhu sādhu! tava saṃskṛtam uttamam asti.
   साधु साधु! तव संस्कृतम् उत्तमम् अस्ति।

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. Wow! This city is very beautiful.
2. What a pity! The rain has come, we can't go.
3. Shame on you! You didn't keep your promise.
4. Well said! Your idea is correct.
5. Long live / Bless you, daughter! May you be victorious.

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

1. **sādhu sādhu** — **sādhu sādhu**! tvaṃ parīkṣāyām prathamaḥ āsīḥ!
2. **dhik** — **dhik** tvām! tvaṃ tasya dhanaṃ coritavān!
3. **hanta** — hā **hanta**! mama priyaḥ śvā mṛtaḥ.
4. **bhoḥ** — **bhoḥ** rāma! atra āgaccha, tvaṃ etat paśya!
5. **asambhavam** — **asambhavam**! etat kartuṃ kasyāpi na śakyam!

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)

**D1.** Sample:

**sītā:** adya mama patiḥ unnatiṃ prāptavān!
**gītā:** aho! adbhutam! sādhu sādhu! ciraṃ jīvatu saḥ!
**sītā:** dhanyavādaḥ! vayaṃ adya utsavaṃ kariṣyāmaḥ.
**gītā:** śobhanam! ahaṃ api āgamiṣyāmi!

*(Sita: Today my husband got a promotion! / Gita: Oh! Amazing! Bravo! May he live long! / Sita: Thank you! We'll celebrate today. / Gita: Excellent! I'll come too!)*

**D2.** Sample:

**rāmaḥ:** śrutaṃ kim? vidyālayaḥ māsatrayaṃ pihitaḥ bhavati.
**hariḥ:** asambhavam! kiṃ etat satyam? hā hanta!
**rāmaḥ:** ām satyam. sarvāḥ parīkṣāḥ api sthagitāḥ.
**hariḥ:** kaṣṭam! vayaṃ bahu pariśramam akurmahi, idānīṃ sarvam vyartham!

*(Ram: Did you hear? The school will be closed for three months. / Hari: Impossible! Is this true? Oh no! / Ram: Yes, true. All exams are also postponed. / Hari: What a pity! We worked so hard, now it's all in vain!)*

## Section E: Free Response — Sample Answers (4 marks)

**E1.** adya sītā dhāvana-spardhāyāṃ prathamā āsīt. sādhu sādhu! sā atīva śīghraṃ dhāvitavatī. aho! sarve janāḥ "adbhutam! adbhutam!" iti avocan. guruḥ avadat "śobhanam! ciraṃ jīva, putrī!"

*(Today Sita came first in the running competition. Bravo! She ran very fast. Oh! All the people said "Amazing! Amazing!" The teacher said, "Excellent! Bless you, daughter!")*

**E2.** śrutaṃ mayā yat mama priyaṃ bhojanaālayaṃ pihitam. kaṣṭam! daśa-varṣebhyaḥ tatra gacchāmi sma. hā hanta! asambhavam etat. kintu diṣṭyā nūtanaṃ bhojanaālayam udghaṭitam — aho, tatra bhojanaṃ śreṣṭham iti śrutam!

*(I heard that my favorite restaurant has closed. What a pity! I'd been going there for ten years. Alas! Unbelievable. But thankfully a new restaurant has opened — oh, I heard the food there is excellent!)*

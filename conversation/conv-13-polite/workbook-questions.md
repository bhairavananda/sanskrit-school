# Stage 13: Polite Phrases — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks, 2 each)

Use Devanagari or IAST. Use the polite phrases from this stage.

1. Please speak slowly.
2. Thank you for the food.
3. Sorry, I am late.
4. Welcome to the school.
5. Good wishes for the festival!

---

3. kṣamyatāṃ mama doṣāya.

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks, 2 each)

1. _______ idaṃ dvāraṃ udgāṭayatu. (Please open this door.)
2. tava upadeśāya _______. (Thank you for your advice.)
3. _______, ahaṃ bhraṣṭavān. (Sorry, I made a mistake.)
4. bhavataḥ gṛhe _______! (Welcome to your home!)
5. _______ prātaḥ daśavādane. (Let us meet again at 10 in the morning.)

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (5 marks, 2.5 each)

Write a 3-4 line dialogue in Sanskrit (IAST is fine) for each situation. Use at least two polite phrases per dialogue.

1. **Situation:** You arrive at a friend's celebration. Greet them, offer good wishes, and thank them for the invitation.

2. **Situation:** You accidentally step on someone's foot in a market. Apologize, check if they are okay, and say goodbye politely.

---

## Section E: Free Response (5 marks, 2.5 each)

1. Write 3-4 sentences describing the most formal way you would greet a respected teacher (*guruḥ*) in Sanskrit. Use as many polite phrases as you can.

2. In your opinion, why does Sanskrit use a passive imperative (*kṣamyatām*) for "sorry" rather than a direct command? Write 2-3 sentences reflecting on this — in English or Sanskrit.

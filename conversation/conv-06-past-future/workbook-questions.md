# Stage 6: Did & Will Do — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (5 x 2 = 10 marks)

Write each sentence in IAST transliteration.

1. I went yesterday.
2. What will you do tomorrow?
3. Did you read the book?
4. She will go to school tomorrow.
5. I wrote a letter.

---

## Section B: Translate to English (5 x 2 = 10 marks)

1. aham bhojanam akhādam.
2. saḥ hyaḥ agacchat.
3. tvam kim kariṣyasi?
4. sā śvaḥ paṭhiṣyati.
5. kim tvam tat apaśyaḥ?

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (5 x 1 = 5 marks)

Choose the correct verb form (past or future as indicated).

1. aham hyaḥ vidyālayam ______. (I went to school yesterday.) [gacch-, past]
2. sā śvaḥ pustakam ______. (She will read a book tomorrow.) [paṭh-, future]
3. kim tvam bhojanam ______? (Did you eat food?) [khād-, past]
4. aham śvaḥ patram ______. (I will write a letter tomorrow.) [likh-, future]
5. saḥ jalam ______. (He drank water.) [pib-, past]

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (2 x 5 = 10 marks)

Write each dialogue in IAST transliteration (3-4 lines each).

1. **Prompt:** Two friends discuss what they did yesterday and what they will do tomorrow. Use at least two past tense and two future tense verbs. Include time markers (**hyaḥ**, **śvaḥ**).

2. **Prompt:** A parent asks a child whether they completed various tasks (eating, reading, writing). The child answers some with "yes, I did" and one with "no, I will do it tomorrow." Use the **kim tvam ... ?** question pattern.

---

## Section E: Free Response (2 x 2.5 = 5 marks)

1. Write about your yesterday in four sentences using past tense, then about your tomorrow in four sentences using future tense.

2. Write a short scene (4-5 lines) where someone recounts an exciting day they had, using at least four different past tense verbs.

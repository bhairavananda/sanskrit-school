# Stage 6: Did & Will Do — Workbook Answers

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit

1. aham hyaḥ agaccham.
2. tvam śvaḥ kim kariṣyasi?
3. kim tvam pustakam apaṭhaḥ?
4. sā śvaḥ vidyālayam gamiṣyati.
5. aham patram alikham.

---

## Section B: Translate to English

1. I ate food.
2. He went yesterday.
3. What will you do?
4. She will read tomorrow.
5. Did you see that?

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank

1. aham hyaḥ vidyālayam **agaccham**.
2. sā śvaḥ pustakam **paṭhiṣyati**.
3. kim tvam bhojanam **akhādaḥ**?
4. aham śvaḥ patram **lekhiṣyāmi**.
5. saḥ jalam **apibat**.

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue — Sample Answers

**Dialogue 1 (Friends discussing yesterday and tomorrow):**

rāmaḥ: tvam hyaḥ kim akārṣīḥ?
sītā: aham hyaḥ pustakam apaṭham. tvam?
rāmaḥ: aham patram alikham. śvaḥ kim kariṣyasi?
sītā: aham śvaḥ vidyālayam gamiṣyāmi.
rāmaḥ: aham api gamiṣyāmi.

**Dialogue 2 (Parent checking on tasks):**

mātā: kim tvam bhojanam akhādaḥ?
bālakaḥ: ām, aham bhojanam akhādam.
mātā: kim tvam pustakam apaṭhaḥ?
bālakaḥ: ām, aham pustakam apaṭham.
mātā: kim tvam patram alikhaḥ?
bālakaḥ: na, aham śvaḥ lekhiṣyāmi.

---

## Section E: Free Response — Sample Answers

**1. Yesterday and tomorrow:**

hyaḥ aham prātaḥ agaccham. aham pustakam apaṭham. aham bhojanam akhādam. aham patram alikham.
śvaḥ aham vidyālayam gamiṣyāmi. aham saṃskṛtam paṭhiṣyāmi. aham bhojanam kariṣyāmi. aham patram lekhiṣyāmi.

**2. Exciting day:**

rāmaḥ: aham hyaḥ prātaḥ agaccham. aham nagaram apaśyam. aham bhojanam akhādam. aham bahūni janān avadam. aham sāyam gṛham agaccham.

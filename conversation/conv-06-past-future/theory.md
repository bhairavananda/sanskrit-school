# Stage 6: Did & Will Do

**Pattern Name:** aham X akārṣam / aham Y kariṣyāmi

---

## The Pattern

| Sanskrit | IAST | Meaning |
|----------|------|---------|
| अहम् X अकार्षम् | aham X akārṣam | I did X |
| अहम् Y करिष्यामि | aham Y kariṣyāmi | I will do Y |
| किम् त्वम् Z अकार्षीः? | kim tvam Z akārṣīḥ? | Did you do Z? |

You already know present tense. Now you get two more gears: reverse (past) and forward (future). Same verbs, different endings.

---

## How It Works

**Past tense (laṅ lakāra)** adds the prefix **a-** to the verb stem and changes the ending. For **karoti** (does): **akārṣam** (I did), **akārṣīḥ** (you did), **akārṣīt** (he/she did). For simpler verbs like **gacchati** (goes): **agaccham** (I went), **agacchaḥ** (you went), **agacchat** (he/she went).

**Future tense (lṛṭ lakāra)** inserts **-iṣya-** or **-sya-** before the ending. For **karoti**: **kariṣyāmi** (I will do), **kariṣyasi** (you will do), **kariṣyati** (he/she will do). For **gacchati**: **gamiṣyāmi** (I will go), **gamiṣyasi** (you will go), **gamiṣyati** (he/she will go).

Here are the key verbs in all three tenses:

| Meaning | Present (I) | Past (I) | Future (I) |
|---------|------------|----------|------------|
| do | karomi | akārṣam | kariṣyāmi |
| go | gacchāmi | agaccham | gamiṣyāmi |
| read | paṭhāmi | apaṭham | paṭhiṣyāmi |
| eat | khādāmi | akhādam | khādiṣyāmi |
| drink | pibāmi | apibam | pāsyāmi |
| write | likhāmi | alikham | lekhiṣyāmi |
| speak | vadāmi | avadam | vadiṣyāmi |
| see | paśyāmi | apaśyam | drakṣyāmi |

## Example Phrases

1. अहम् भोजनम् अकार्षम्।
   aham bhojanam akārṣam.
   "I made food." / "I did (the) food."

2. अहम् श्वः गमिष्यामि।
   aham śvaḥ gamiṣyāmi.
   "I will go tomorrow."

3. किम् त्वम् पुस्तकम् अपठः?
   kim tvam pustakam apaṭhaḥ?
   "Did you read the book?"

4. सः ह्यः अगच्छत्।
   saḥ hyaḥ agacchat.
   "He went yesterday."

5. सा श्वः पठिष्यति।
   sā śvaḥ paṭhiṣyati.
   "She will read tomorrow."

6. अहम् पत्रम् अलिखम्।
   aham patram alikham.
   "I wrote a letter."

7. त्वम् किम् करिष्यसि?
   tvam kim kariṣyasi?
   "What will you do?"

8. अहम् ह्यः जलम् अपिबम्।
   aham hyaḥ jalam apibam.
   "I drank water yesterday."

9. सः भोजनम् खादिष्यति।
   saḥ bhojanam khādiṣyati.
   "He will eat food."

10. किम् त्वम् तत् अपश्यः?
    kim tvam tat apaśyaḥ?
    "Did you see that?"

## Mini-Dialogue 1

**रामः (rāmaḥ):** त्वम् ह्यः किम् अकार्षीः?
tvam hyaḥ kim akārṣīḥ?
"What did you do yesterday?"

**सीता (sītā):** अहम् पुस्तकम् अपठम्। त्वम् किम् अकार्षीः?
aham pustakam apaṭham. tvam kim akārṣīḥ?
"I read a book. What did you do?"

**रामः:** अहम् पत्रम् अलिखम्।
aham patram alikham.
"I wrote a letter."

**सीता:** श्वः किम् करिष्यसि?
śvaḥ kim kariṣyasi?
"What will you do tomorrow?"

**रामः:** अहम् विद्यालयम् गमिष्यामि।
aham vidyālayam gamiṣyāmi.
"I will go to school."

## Mini-Dialogue 2

**माता (mātā):** किम् त्वम् भोजनम् अखादः?
kim tvam bhojanam akhādaḥ?
"Did you eat food?"

**बालकः (bālakaḥ):** आम्, अहम् भोजनम् अखादम्।
ām, aham bhojanam akhādam.
"Yes, I ate food."

**माता:** किम् त्वम् दुग्धम् अपिबः?
kim tvam dugdham apibaḥ?
"Did you drink milk?"

**बालकः:** न! अहम् श्वः पास्यामि।
na! aham śvaḥ pāsyāmi.
"No! I will drink (it) tomorrow."

---

## Variations

1. **Time markers:** Use **hyaḥ** (yesterday), **adya** (today), **śvaḥ** (tomorrow), **prātaḥ** (in the morning), **sāyam** (in the evening) to anchor your sentences in time.

2. **"Just did" with samprati:** **aham samprati āgaccham** = "I just arrived." The word **samprati** ("just now") bridges present and past.

3. **"About to" with the future:** The future tense can express immediate intention: **aham idānīm gacchāmi** (present = "I'm going now") vs. **aham idānīm gamiṣyāmi** (future = "I'm about to go").

---

## Common Mistakes

1. **Forgetting the a- prefix in past tense.** Past tense verbs get the augment **a-** at the front: **agaccham**, not **gaccham**. Without it, you're back in present tense territory.

2. **Mixing up past and future endings.** Past tense has short, clipped endings (**-am**, **-aḥ**, **-at**). Future tense has the **-iṣya-/-sya-** marker. If you hear that **-ṣya-** sound, it's future.

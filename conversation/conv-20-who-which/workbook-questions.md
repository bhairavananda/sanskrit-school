# Stage 20: The One Who... — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks, 2 each)

1. He who speaks the truth is happy.
2. The book which is on the table is mine.
3. The girl who is singing is my daughter.
4. The food which you cook is delicious.
5. Those who help others obtain peace.

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks, 2 each)

1. यः विद्यां लभते सः धनवान् भवति।
   *yaḥ vidyāṃ labhate saḥ dhanavān bhavati.*

2. यत् गृहं त्वं पश्यसि तत् नवीनम् अस्ति।
   *yat gṛhaṃ tvaṃ paśyasi tat navīnam asti.*

3. या नदी अत्र वहति सा गङ्गा अस्ति।
   *yā nadī atra vahati sā gaṅgā asti.*

4. ये छात्राः परिश्रमं कुर्वन्ति ते उत्तीर्णाः भवन्ति।
   *ye chātrāḥ pariśramaṃ kurvanti te uttīrṇāḥ bhavanti.*

5. यस्मिन् वने अहं गच्छामि तस्मिन् वने व्याघ्राः सन्ति।
   *yasmin vane ahaṃ gacchāmi tasmin vane vyāghrāḥ santi.*

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks, 2 each)

1. __________ जनः परोपकारं करोति सः सुखी भवति। (supply the masculine relative pronoun)
   *__________ janaḥ paropakāraṃ karoti saḥ sukhī bhavati.*

2. या बालिका अत्र पठति __________ मम सखी अस्ति। (supply the feminine correlative)
   *yā bālikā atra paṭhati __________ mama sakhī asti.*

3. यत् पुस्तकं मम हस्ते अस्ति __________ रोचकम् अस्ति। (supply the neuter correlative)
   *yat pustakaṃ mama haste asti __________ rocakam asti.*

4. __________ मार्गेण त्वं गच्छसि तेन मार्गेण अहम् अपि गच्छामि। (supply the instrumental relative)
   *__________ mārgeṇa tvaṃ gacchasi tena mārgeṇa aham api gacchāmi.*

5. ये बालकाः क्रीडन्ति __________ मम मित्राणि सन्ति। (supply the masculine plural correlative)
   *ye bālakāḥ krīḍanti __________ mama mitrāṇi santi.*

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks, 3 each)

1. **Prompt:** Someone points at two people in a crowd and asks who they are. The other person identifies each using *yaḥ...saḥ* and *yā...sā*. Write 3-4 lines.

2. **Prompt:** A teacher shares a proverb using the *yaḥ...saḥ* pattern, and a student responds with their own proverb in the same pattern. Write 3-4 lines.

---

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks, 2 each)

1. Write a proverb or wise saying using the *yaḥ...saḥ* pattern. Then explain in English what it means.

2. Describe your home or school using the *yasmin...tasmin* (locative relative) pattern. Write 2-3 sentences in Sanskrit.

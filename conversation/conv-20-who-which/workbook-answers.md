# Stage 20: The One Who... — Workbook Answers

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit

1. **यः सत्यं वदति सः सुखी अस्ति।**
   *yaḥ satyaṃ vadati saḥ sukhī asti.*

2. **यत् पुस्तकं मेषोपरि अस्ति तत् मम अस्ति।**
   *yat pustakaṃ meṣopari asti tat mama asti.*

3. **या बालिका गायति सा मम पुत्री अस्ति।**
   *yā bālikā gāyati sā mama putrī asti.*

4. **यत् भोजनं त्वं पचसि तत् स्वादिष्टम् अस्ति।**
   *yat bhojanaṃ tvaṃ pacasi tat svādiṣṭam asti.*

5. **ये अन्यान् सहायन्ते ते शान्तिं लभन्ते।**
   *ye anyān sahāyante te śāntiṃ labhante.*

---

## Section B: Translate to English

1. He who obtains knowledge becomes wealthy.

2. The house which you see is new.

3. The river which flows here is the Ganga.

4. Those students who work hard pass (the exam).

5. In the forest where I go, there are tigers.

## Section C: Fill in the Blank

1. **यः** (*yaḥ*) — masculine singular relative pronoun

2. **सा** (*sā*) — feminine singular correlative pronoun

3. **तत्** (*tat*) — neuter singular correlative pronoun

4. **येन** (*yena*) — instrumental singular relative pronoun

5. **ते** (*te*) — masculine plural correlative pronoun

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue

**1. In a crowd:**

-- तौ द्वौ जनौ कौ स्तः?
   *tau dvau janau kau staḥ?*
   Who are those two people?

-- यः पुरुषः श्वेतं वस्त्रं धारयति सः मम पिता अस्ति।
   *yaḥ puruṣaḥ śvetaṃ vastraṃ dhārayati saḥ mama pitā asti.*
   The man who is wearing white clothes is my father.

-- या स्त्री तस्य समीपे तिष्ठति सा कः?
   *yā strī tasya samīpe tiṣṭhati sā kaḥ?*
   The woman who stands near him -- who is she?

-- सा मम माता अस्ति।
   *sā mama mātā asti.*
   She is my mother.

**2. Proverb exchange:**

-- छात्राः, एकं सूक्तिं शृणुत — यः धर्मं रक्षति सः धर्मेण रक्ष्यते।
   *chātrāḥ, ekaṃ sūktiṃ śṛṇuta — yaḥ dharmaṃ rakṣati saḥ dharmeṇa rakṣyate.*
   Students, hear a proverb — He who protects dharma is protected by dharma.

-- गुरो, अहम् अपि एकं जानामि — या विद्या मुक्तिं ददाति सा एव उत्तमा विद्या।
   *guro, aham api ekaṃ jānāmi — yā vidyā muktiṃ dadāti sā eva uttamā vidyā.*
   Teacher, I also know one — The knowledge which gives liberation is the best knowledge.

-- साधु! सम्यक् उक्तम्।
   *sādhu! samyak uktam.*
   Excellent! Well said.

## Section E: Free Response (Sample Answers)

**1.**
यः क्षमां करोति सः वीरः अस्ति।
*yaḥ kṣamāṃ karoti saḥ vīraḥ asti.*
He who forgives is a hero. — This proverb means that true strength is shown not in fighting but in the ability to forgive.

**2.**
यस्मिन् नगरे अहं वसामि तस्मिन् नगरे बहूनि मन्दिराणि सन्ति। यस्मिन् गृहे अहं पठामि तस्मिन् गृहे एकम् उद्यानम् अस्ति। तत् उद्यानं सुन्दरम् अस्ति।
*yasmin nagare ahaṃ vasāmi tasmin nagare bahūni mandirāṇi santi. yasmin gṛhe ahaṃ paṭhāmi tasmin gṛhe ekam udyānam asti. tat udyānaṃ sundaram asti.*
In the city where I live, there are many temples. In the house where I study, there is a garden. That garden is beautiful.

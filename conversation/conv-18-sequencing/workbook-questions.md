# Stage 18: First, Then, Finally — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks, 2 each)

Use prathamam, tataḥ, anantaram, ante/antataḥ, and pūrvam as appropriate.

1. First open the book, then read the lesson.
2. Before sleeping, I drink warm milk.
3. He first went to the market, then to the temple, and finally came home.
4. After eating, we walked in the garden.
5. Earlier I did not know Sanskrit, but now I speak it.

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks, 2 each)

1. prathamaṃ snānaṃ kuru, tataḥ pūjāṃ kuru.
2. bhojanāt pūrvaṃ hastau prakṣālaya.
3. saḥ prathamaṃ hasitavān, tataḥ roditavān, ante tu nirgatavān.
4. anantaraṃ vayaṃ nagaraṃ draṣṭuṃ gatavantaḥ.
5. ādau kaṣṭam āsīt, paścāt sulabham abhavat.

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks, 2 each)

1. _______ jalaṃ uṣṇaṃ kuru, _______ cāya-patrāṇi yojaya. (First heat the water, then add tea leaves.)
2. snānāt _______ vastrāṇi dhārayāmi. (After bathing, I wear clothes.)
3. _______ sā grāme avasata. (Earlier, she lived in a village.)
4. prathamaṃ śṛṇu, tataḥ vicāraya, _______ vada. (First listen, then think, finally speak.)
5. vidyālayāt _______ gṛhakāryaṃ karomi. (After school, I do homework.)

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (5 marks, 2.5 each)

Write a 3-4 line dialogue in Sanskrit (IAST is fine) for each situation.

1. **Situation:** Someone asks you how to make tea (cāyam). Give step-by-step instructions using prathamam, tataḥ, anantaram, and antataḥ.

2. **Situation:** A friend asks what you did yesterday (hyaḥ). Narrate your day in order using sequencing words.

---

## Section E: Free Response (5 marks, 2.5 each)

1. Describe the route from your home to a place you visit often (school, temple, market). Use at least three sequencing words and give directions in Sanskrit (3-4 sentences).

2. The phrase *prathamaṃ śrutvā tataḥ vicintya anantaraṃ vadatu* ("first listen, then think, then speak") is practical advice. Write your own 3-step advice in Sanskrit using sequencing words, and explain why you chose those three steps.

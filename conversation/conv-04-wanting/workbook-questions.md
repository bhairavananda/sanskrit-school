# Stage 4: Wanting & Needing — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (5 x 2 = 10 marks)

Write each sentence in IAST transliteration.

1. I want water.
2. Do you want tea?
3. She wants a book.
4. I need food.
5. I need rest.

---

## Section B: Translate to English (5 x 2 = 10 marks)

1. aham phalam icchāmi.
2. kim tvam dugdham icchasi?
3. saḥ sahāyam icchati.
4. mama jalam āvaśyakam.
5. aham bhojanam icchāmi.

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (5 x 1 = 5 marks)

1. aham jalam ______. (I want water.)
2. kim tvam phalam ______? (Do you want fruit?)
3. mama viśrāmaḥ ______. (I need rest.)
4. sā ______ icchati. (She wants a book.)
5. ______ bhojanam āvaśyakam. (I need food.)

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (2 x 5 = 10 marks)

Write each dialogue in IAST transliteration (3-4 lines each).

1. **Prompt:** You are at a friend's house. They offer you tea, but you want water. Then they offer fruit, and you accept. Use **kim tvam ... icchasi?** and **aham ... icchāmi**.

2. **Prompt:** A child tells a parent what they need (food, help with something). The parent asks follow-up questions. Use both **icchāmi** and **āvaśyakam** patterns.

---

## Section E: Free Response (2 x 2.5 = 5 marks)

1. Write four sentences: two things you want right now (using **icchāmi**) and two things you need (using **āvaśyakam**).

2. Write a short scene (4-5 lines) at a market where a buyer asks for various items using the wanting/needing patterns.

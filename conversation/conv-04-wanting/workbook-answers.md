# Stage 4: Wanting & Needing — Workbook Answers

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit

1. aham jalam icchāmi.
2. kim tvam cāyam icchasi?
3. sā pustakam icchati.
4. mama bhojanam āvaśyakam.
5. mama viśrāmaḥ āvaśyakaḥ.

---

## Section B: Translate to English

1. I want a fruit.
2. Do you want milk?
3. He wants help.
4. I need water.
5. I want food.

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank

1. aham jalam **icchāmi**.
2. kim tvam phalam **icchasi**?
3. mama viśrāmaḥ **āvaśyakaḥ**.
4. sā **pustakam** icchati.
5. **mama** bhojanam āvaśyakam.

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue — Sample Answers

**Dialogue 1 (At a friend's house):**

mitraḥ: kim tvam cāyam icchasi?
aham: na, aham jalam icchāmi.
mitraḥ: kim tvam phalam icchasi?
aham: ām, aham phalam icchāmi.

**Dialogue 2 (Child and parent):**

bālakaḥ: mama bhojanam āvaśyakam. aham kṣudhitaḥ asmi.
mātā: kim tvam phalam icchasi?
bālakaḥ: na, aham bhojanam icchāmi. mama sahāyaḥ api āvaśyakaḥ.
mātā: kim sahāyam? pustakam paṭhitum?

---

## Section E: Free Response — Sample Answers

**1. Wants and needs:**

aham cāyam icchāmi. aham pustakam icchāmi.
mama viśrāmaḥ āvaśyakaḥ. mama jalam āvaśyakam.

**2. Market scene:**

kretā: aham phalam icchāmi.
vikretā: kim tvam āmram icchasi?
kretā: ām, āmram icchāmi. mama dugdham api āvaśyakam.
vikretā: dugdham atra asti.
kretā: aham dugdham api icchāmi.

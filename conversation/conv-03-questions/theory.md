# Stage 3: Questions

**Pattern Name:** kim / kaḥ / kutra / kadā / katham / kasmāt

---

## The Pattern

| Sanskrit | IAST | Meaning |
|----------|------|---------|
| किम् | kim | What? |
| कः / का | kaḥ / kā | Who? (masc. / fem.) |
| कुत्र | kutra | Where? |
| कदा | kadā | When? |
| कथम् | katham | How? |
| कस्मात् / किमर्थम् | kasmāt / kimartham | Why? |

Every question word starts with **ka-**. That's your anchor. Learn the **ka-** family and you can ask about anything.

---

## How It Works

Sanskrit question words slot into the same position as the answer would occupy. English moves the question word to the front ("Where is the book?"), but Sanskrit keeps it in place: **pustakam kutra asti?** — literally "book where is?" The word order is flexible, but keeping the question word near the verb sounds natural.

For "who," gender matters: **kaḥ** asks about a male, **kā** about a female. **kim** does double duty — it means "what?" on its own, and also serves as the neuter form of "who/which." Context makes it clear.

---

## Example Phrases

1. एतत् किम् अस्ति?
   etat kim asti?
   "What is this?"

2. सः कः अस्ति?
   saḥ kaḥ asti?
   "Who is he?"

3. सा का अस्ति?
   sā kā asti?
   "Who is she?"

4. रामः कुत्र अस्ति?
   rāmaḥ kutra asti?
   "Where is Rama?"

5. भोजनम् कदा अस्ति?
   bhojanam kadā asti?
   "When is the meal?"

6. एतत् कथम् अस्ति?
   etat katham asti?
   "How is this?" / "What is this like?"

7. त्वम् कुत्र असि?
   tvam kutra asi?
   "Where are you?"

8. त्वम् कस्मात् श्रान्तः असि?
   tvam kasmāt śrāntaḥ asi?
   "Why are you tired?"

9. विद्यालयः कुत्र अस्ति?
   vidyālayaḥ kutra asti?
   "Where is the school?"

10. तव नाम किम् अस्ति?
    tava nāma kim asti?
    "What is your name?"

---

## Mini-Dialogue 1

**रामः (rāmaḥ):** तव नाम किम् अस्ति?
tava nāma kim asti?
"What is your name?"

**सीता (sītā):** मम नाम सीता अस्ति। तव नाम किम्?
mama nāma sītā asti. tava nāma kim?
"My name is Sita. What's yours?"

**रामः:** मम नाम रामः। त्वम् कुत्र असि?
mama nāma rāmaḥ. tvam kutra asi?
"My name is Rama. Where are you (from)?"

**सीता:** अहम् अयोध्यायाम् अस्मि।
aham ayodhyāyām asmi.
"I am in Ayodhya."

## Mini-Dialogue 2

**बालकः (bālakaḥ):** भोजनम् कदा अस्ति?
bhojanam kadā asti?
"When is the meal?"

**माता (mātā):** शीघ्रम्। त्वम् कस्मात् क्षुधितः असि?
śīghram. tvam kasmāt kṣudhitaḥ asi?
"Soon. Why are you hungry?"

**बालकः:** अहम् बहु क्रीडितवान्!
aham bahu krīḍitavān!
"I played a lot!"

**माता:** तत् फलम् खाद।
tat phalam khāda.
"Eat that fruit."

---

## Variations

1. **kim + noun = "which?":** When **kim** sits before a noun, it becomes "which" — **kim pustakam?** = "Which book?" The form adjusts for gender: **kaḥ bālakaḥ?** = "Which boy?", **kā bālikā?** = "Which girl?"

2. **Rhetorical questions with kim:** Adding **kim** at the start of any statement turns it into a yes/no question: **kim tvam chātraḥ asi?** = "Are you a student?"

3. **kiyat = "how much/many?":** A cousin of the **ka-** family: **kiyat dūram?** = "How far?"

---

## Common Mistakes

1. **Using kim for "who" with people.** For "who is he?", use **kaḥ** (not **kim**): **saḥ kaḥ asti?** The word **kim** is for "what" or neuter "which."

2. **Forgetting gender on kaḥ/kā.** Asking about a woman with **kaḥ** is like saying "who is he?" when you mean "she." Use **kā** for feminine.

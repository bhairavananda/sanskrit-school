# Stage 3: Questions — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (5 x 2 = 10 marks)

Write each sentence in IAST transliteration.

1. What is your name?
2. Where is the school?
3. Who is she?
4. When is the meal?
5. Why are you tired? (to a male)

---

## Section B: Translate to English (5 x 2 = 10 marks)

1. saḥ kaḥ asti?
2. tvam kutra asi?
3. etat kim asti?
4. bhojanam kadā asti?
5. tvam kasmāt kṣudhitaḥ asi?

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (5 x 1 = 5 marks)

Choose the correct question word: kim, kaḥ, kā, kutra, kadā, katham, kasmāt.

1. tava nāma ______ asti? (What is your name?)
2. rāmaḥ ______ asti? (Where is Rama?)
3. sā ______ asti? (Who is she?)
4. bhojanam ______ asti? (When is the meal?)
5. tvam ______ śrāntaḥ asi? (Why are you tired?)

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (2 x 5 = 10 marks)

Write each dialogue in IAST transliteration (3-4 lines each).

1. **Prompt:** Two people meet at a gathering. They ask each other's names and where they are from. Use at least **kim**, **kutra**, and one other question word.

2. **Prompt:** A child bombards a parent with questions — "what is that?", "where is it?", "why?" The parent answers patiently. Use at least three different question words.

---

## Section E: Free Response (2 x 2.5 = 5 marks)

1. Write five different questions you might ask a new friend, each using a different question word from this stage.

2. Imagine you arrive in a new city. Write 3-4 questions you would need to ask, using the patterns from this stage.

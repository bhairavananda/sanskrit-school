# Stage 3: Questions — Workbook Answers

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit

1. tava nāma kim asti?
2. vidyālayaḥ kutra asti?
3. sā kā asti?
4. bhojanam kadā asti?
5. tvam kasmāt śrāntaḥ asi?

---

## Section B: Translate to English

1. Who is he?
2. Where are you?
3. What is this?
4. When is the meal?
5. Why are you hungry?

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue — Sample Answers

**Dialogue 1 (At a gathering):**

rāmaḥ: tava nāma kim asti?
sītā: mama nāma sītā asti. tvam kutra asi?
rāmaḥ: aham ayodhyāyām asmi. tvam kā asi?
sītā: aham adhyāpikā asmi.

**Dialogue 2 (Child questioning parent):**

bālakaḥ: tat kim asti?
pitā: tat candraḥ asti.
bālakaḥ: saḥ kutra asti?
pitā: saḥ ākāśe asti.
bālakaḥ: kasmāt saḥ tatra asti?
pitā: sā tasya sthānam asti!

---

## Section E: Free Response — Sample Answers

**1. Five questions for a new friend:**

tava nāma kim asti?
tvam kutra asi?
tvam kaḥ asi? *(What do you do?)*
tvam katham asi? *(How are you?)*
tvam kadā āgataḥ? *(When did you come?)*

**2. Questions in a new city:**

vidyālayaḥ kutra asti?
bhojanam kutra asti?
bhojanam kadā asti?
etat nagaram kim asti? *(What is this city?)*

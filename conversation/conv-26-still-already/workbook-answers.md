# Stage 26: Still / Already / No Longer / Not Yet — Answer Key

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

1. अहम् अधुनापि प्रतीक्षे। (aham adhunāpi pratīkṣe.)
2. जलं पूर्वम् एव उष्णम्। (jalaṃ pūrvam eva uṣṇam.)
3. सः न पुनः अत्र पाठयति। (saḥ na punaḥ atra pāṭhayati.)
4. पत्रं न यावत् आगतम्। (patraṃ na yāvat āgatam.)
5. किं त्वम् अधुनापि स्वपिषि? (kiṃ tvam adhunāpi svapiṣi?)

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. She is still singing.
2. The fruits have already been bought.
3. They no longer come here.
4. The tree has not blossomed yet.
5. Are you still there?

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

1. **अधुनापि** (adhunāpi) — He still studies at school.
2. **पूर्वम् एव** (pūrvam eva) — I already did the homework.
3. **न तावत्** (na tāvat) — The sun has not risen yet.
4. **न पुनः** (na punaḥ) — Those two are no longer friends.
5. **अधुनापि** (adhunāpi) — Is it still raining?

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (5 marks)

**Prompt 1 — Sample:**

> **यात्री** — यानं किम् आगतम्? (yānaṃ kim āgatam?)
> "Has the bus arrived?"
>
> **जनः** — न यावत्। किन्तु समयः पूर्वम् एव अतीतः। (na yāvat. kintu samayaḥ pūrvam eva atītaḥ.)
> "Not yet. But the time has already passed."
>
> **यात्री** — अहम् अधुनापि प्रतीक्षे। (aham adhunāpi pratīkṣe.)
> "I'm still waiting."
>
> **जनः** — धैर्यं धारय। आगमिष्यति। (dhairyaṃ dhāraya. āgamiṣyati.)
> "Be patient. It will come."

**Prompt 2 — Sample:**

> **क:** सः न पुनः नगरे वसति। (saḥ na punaḥ nagare vasati.)
> "He no longer lives in the city."
>
> **ख:** सत्यम्? किं सः अधुनापि ग्रामे अस्ति? (satyam? kiṃ saḥ adhunāpi grāme asti?)
> "Really? Is he still in the village?"
>
> **क:** आम्, सः प्राग् एव तत्र गतवान्। (ām, saḥ prāg eva tatra gatavān.)
> "Yes, he already went there."
>
> **ख:** अहो, ग्रामजीवनं खलु सुखकरम्। (aho, grāmajīvanaṃ khalu sukhakaram.)
> "Well, village life is certainly pleasant."

## Section E: Free Response — Sample Answers (5 marks)

**1. Morning routine:**

- प्रातः अहं पूर्वम् एव उत्थितः। (prātaḥ ahaṃ pūrvam eva utthitaḥ.) — "In the morning I've already woken up."
- किन्तु चायं न तावत् पीतम्। (kintu cāyaṃ na tāvat pītam.) — "But I haven't had tea yet."
- मम भ्राता अधुनापि शय्यायां तिष्ठति। (mama bhrātā adhunāpi śayyāyāṃ tiṣṭhati.) — "My brother is still in bed."

**2. No longer / still:**

- अहं न पुनः मांसं खादामि। (ahaṃ na punaḥ māṃsaṃ khādāmi.) — "I no longer eat meat."
- अहम् अधुनापि प्रतिदिनं योगं करोमि। (aham adhunāpi pratidinaṃ yogaṃ karomi.) — "I still practice yoga every day."

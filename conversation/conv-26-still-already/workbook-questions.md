# Stage 26: Still / Already / No Longer / Not Yet — Workbook

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

Use Devanagari and IAST.

1. I am still waiting.
2. The water is already hot.
3. He no longer teaches here.
4. The letter has not arrived yet.
5. Are you still sleeping?

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. सा अधुनापि गायति। (sā adhunāpi gāyati.)
2. फलानि पूर्वम् एव क्रीतानि। (phalāni pūrvam eva krītāni.)
3. ते न पुनः अत्र आगच्छन्ति। (te na punaḥ atra āgacchanti.)
4. वृक्षः न तावत् पुष्पितः। (vṛkṣaḥ na tāvat puṣpitaḥ.)
5. किं त्वम् अधुनापि तत्र एव असि? (kiṃ tvam adhunāpi tatra eva asi?)

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

Fill in with adhunāpi, pūrvam eva, na punaḥ, or na yāvat / na tāvat.

1. सः ___ विद्यालये पठति। (He still studies at school.)
2. अहं ___ गृहकार्यं कृतवान्। (I already did the homework.)
3. सूर्यः ___ उदितः। (The sun has not risen yet.)
4. तौ ___ मित्रे स्तः। (Those two are no longer friends.)
5. किं वर्षा ___ चलति? (Is it still raining?)

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (5 marks)

**Prompt 1:** A traveler asks if the bus has arrived. The other person says "not yet" and adds "but it's already late." The traveler says they are still waiting. Write 3-4 lines.

**Prompt 2:** Two people discuss an old friend. One says "he no longer lives in the city." The other is surprised — "Is he still in the village?" Write 3-4 lines.

---

## Section E: Free Response (5 marks)

1. Describe your morning routine using at least two of these markers (still, already, not yet, no longer). Write 3-4 sentences in Sanskrit with IAST and English translations.

2. Think of something you used to do but no longer do, and something you still do. Write one sentence for each using na punaḥ and adhunāpi.

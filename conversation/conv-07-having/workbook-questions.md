# Stage 7: Having & Existing — Workbook

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)
*Use the mama X asti pattern. Write in IAST.*

1. I have a book.
2. Do you have water?
3. There are fruits here.
4. He does not have time.
5. Who has money?

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. mama mitram atra asti.
2. tava dugdham asti vā?
3. tatra phalāni santi.
4. tasyāḥ gṛham na asti.
5. atra kim asti?

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)
*Fill in the missing word.*

1. ______ pustakam asti. (I have a book.)
2. tava jalam ______ vā? (Do you have water?)
3. atra phalāni ______. (There are fruits here.)
4. mama samayaḥ ______ asti. (I don't have time.)
5. ______ dhanam asti? (Who has money?)

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)
*Write a 3-4 line dialogue in IAST for each prompt.*

1. You are at a shop. Ask the shopkeeper if they have milk. The shopkeeper says yes. You ask if they also have fruits.

2. A friend asks what you have in your bag. You say you have two books and water. Your friend says they have nothing.

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks)

1. Describe a room using atra X asti / atra X santi patterns (3-4 sentences in IAST).

2. Write about what you have and don't have — possessions, time, friends, anything (3-4 sentences in IAST).

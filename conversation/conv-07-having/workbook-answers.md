# Stage 7: Having & Existing — Answer Key

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

1. mama pustakam asti.
2. tava jalam asti vā?
3. atra phalāni santi.
4. tasya samayaḥ na asti.
5. kasya dhanam asti?

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. My friend is here.
2. Do you have milk?
3. There are fruits there.
4. She does not have a house.
5. What is here?

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

1. **mama** pustakam asti.
2. tava jalam **asti** vā?
3. atra phalāni **santi**.
4. mama samayaḥ **na** asti.
5. **kasya** dhanam asti?

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)

### Dialogue 1 (sample)
> A: tava dugdham asti vā?
> B: ām, mama dugdham asti.
> A: phalāni api santi vā?
> B: ām, atra phalāni api santi.

### Dialogue 2 (sample)
> A: tava syūte kim asti?
> B: mama dve pustake staḥ, jalam ca asti.
> A: mama kiñcit na asti.
> B: etat gṛhāṇa — ekaṃ pustakam tava.

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks)

### Sample 1
atra ekaṃ gṛham asti. atra dve pustake staḥ. atra jalam asti. atra phalāni na santi.

### Sample 2
mama pustakam asti. mama mitrāṇi santi. mama samayaḥ na asti. mama dhanam api na asti!

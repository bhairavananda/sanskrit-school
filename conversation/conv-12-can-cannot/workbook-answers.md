# Stage 12: Can & Cannot — Answer Key

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

1. ahaṃ saṃskṛtaṃ vaktuṃ śaknomi.
2. kiṃ tvaṃ śvaḥ āgantuṃ śaknoṣi?
3. sā gātuṃ śaknoti.
4. vayaṃ tatra gantuṃ na śaknumaḥ.
5. tvaṃ kiṃ kartuṃ śaknoṣi?

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. I can do it.
2. He cannot eat.
3. Can you go?
4. We can read.
5. She cannot speak.

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

1. ahaṃ saṃskṛtaṃ **vaktuṃ** śaknomi.
2. tvaṃ gantuṃ **śaknoṣi**?
3. saḥ **khādituṃ** na śaknoti.
4. vayaṃ paṭhituṃ **śaknumaḥ**.
5. ahaṃ sāhāyyaṃ **kartuṃ** śaknomi.

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)

### Dialogue 1 (sample)
> A: udyānaṃ āgaccha! āvāṃ gacchāvaḥ.
> B: adya gantuṃ na śaknomi. śvaḥ śaknomi.
> A: samyak. śvaḥ gacchāvaḥ.
> B: uttamam!

### Dialogue 2 (sample)
> A: tvaṃ saṃskṛtaṃ vaktuṃ śaknoṣi vā?
> B: kiñcit vaktuṃ śaknomi.
> A: paṭhituṃ api śaknoṣi?
> B: na, paṭhituṃ na śaknomi adhunā.

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks)

### Sample 1
ahaṃ saṃskṛtaṃ vaktuṃ śaknomi. ahaṃ gātuṃ śaknomi. ahaṃ paṭhituṃ śaknomi. ahaṃ pāktuṃ śaknomi. ahaṃ taritum na śaknomi. ahaṃ phārasībhāṣāṃ vaktuṃ na śaknomi.

### Sample 2
mitram avadat — "mama bhāraḥ asti, voḍhuṃ na śaknomi." ahaṃ avadaṃ — "ahaṃ sāhāyyaṃ kartuṃ śaknomi. etat mahyaṃ dehi." mitram avadat — "dhanyavādaḥ! tvam uttamaḥ mitraṃ asi."

# Stage 12: Can & Cannot — Workbook

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)
*Write in IAST.*

1. I can speak Sanskrit.
2. Can you come tomorrow?
3. She can sing.
4. We cannot go there.
5. What can you do?

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. ahaṃ kartuṃ śaknomi.
2. saḥ khādituṃ na śaknoti.
3. kiṃ tvaṃ gantuṃ śaknoṣi?
4. vayaṃ paṭhituṃ śaknumaḥ.
5. sā vaktuṃ na śaknoti.

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)
*Fill in the missing word.*

1. ahaṃ saṃskṛtaṃ ______ śaknomi. (I can speak Sanskrit.)
2. tvaṃ gantuṃ ______? (Can you go?)
3. saḥ ______ na śaknoti. (He cannot eat.)
4. vayaṃ paṭhituṃ ______. (We can read.)
5. ahaṃ sāhāyyaṃ ______ śaknomi. (I can help.)

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)
*Write a 3-4 line dialogue in IAST for each prompt.*

1. You invite a friend to come to the garden. They say they can't today but can tomorrow. You agree to go tomorrow.

2. Someone asks if you can speak Sanskrit. You say a little. They ask if you can read too. You say you can't read yet.

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks)

1. List four things you can do and two things you cannot do (4-5 sentences in IAST).

2. Write a short scene where someone offers help — "I can do this for you" — using śaknomi and kartum (3-4 sentences in IAST).

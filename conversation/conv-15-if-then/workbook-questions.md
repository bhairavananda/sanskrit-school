# Stage 15: If & Then — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks, 2 each)

Use yadi...tarhi, cet, or the optative mood as appropriate.

1. If you come tomorrow, then we will play.
2. If I were rich, I would travel the world.
3. If the teacher asks, tell the truth.
4. If there is no water, the plants die.
5. Go quickly, otherwise (nocet) you will be late.

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks, 2 each)

1. yadi tvaṃ jalaṃ pibasi tarhi svāsthyaṃ bhavati.
2. yadi sā āgacchet tarhi vayaṃ kathāṃ śṛṇuyāma.
3. dhanaṃ cet asti tarhi dānaṃ kuru.
4. yadi ahaṃ pakṣī syām tarhi himālayaṃ paśyeyam.
5. śīghraṃ paṭha, nocet parīkṣāyāṃ na utīrṇaḥ bhavasi.

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks, 2 each)

1. _______ vṛṣṭiḥ bhavati _______ kṛṣakāḥ prasannāḥ bhavanti. (If it rains, then the farmers are happy.)
2. tvaṃ _______ āgacchasi tarhi miṣṭānnaṃ dadāmi. (If you come, I will give sweets.)
3. yadi samayaḥ _______ tarhi viśrāmaṃ kuryām. (If there were time, I would rest.)
4. śīghraṃ gaccha, _______ vilambaḥ bhaviṣyati. (Go fast, otherwise there will be a delay.)
5. yadi sā gāyet tarhi sarve _______ . (If she were to sing, everyone would listen.)

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (5 marks, 2.5 each)

Write a 3-4 line dialogue in Sanskrit (IAST is fine) for each situation.

1. **Situation:** Two friends discuss what they would do if they had a flying vehicle (*vimānam*). Use hypothetical conditionals with the optative.

2. **Situation:** A parent warns a child — if you don't eat vegetables, you won't be strong. The child responds with their own "if...then." Use *yadi...tarhi* or *nocet*.

---

## Section E: Free Response (5 marks, 2.5 each)

1. Write 3-4 sentences about what you would do if you could live in any time period. Use *yadi...syāt...tarhi...* constructions.

2. What is the difference between *yadi saḥ gacchati* and *yadi saḥ gacchet*? Explain in 2-3 sentences — English or Sanskrit.

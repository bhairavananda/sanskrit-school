# Stage 23: Would Have / But Instead — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks, 2 each)

1. I would have come but I was busy.
2. If she had studied, she would have passed.
3. We would have eaten there but the restaurant was closed.
4. Instead, I read a book at home.
5. If you had told me, I would have helped.

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks, 2 each)

1. अहम् अक्रेष्यम् किन्तु धनं न आसीत्।
   *aham akreṣyam kintu dhanaṃ na āsīt.*

2. यदि वर्षा न अभविष्यत् तर्हि वयम् अक्रीडिष्याम।
   *yadi varṣā na abhaviṣyat tarhi vayam akrīḍiṣyāma.*

3. सः अवदिष्यत् किन्तु भयम् अनुभूतवान्।
   *saḥ avadiṣyat kintu bhayam anubhūtavān.*

4. तत् स्थाने ते गृहे अतिष्ठन्।
   *tat sthāne te gṛhe atiṣṭhan.*

5. यदि मार्गः शुद्धः अभविष्यत् तर्हि शीघ्रम् आगमिष्याम।
   *yadi mārgaḥ śuddhaḥ abhaviṣyat tarhi śīghram āgamiṣyāma.*

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks, 2 each)

1. अहम् __________ किन्तु समयः न आसीत्। (I would have gone — supply conditional of gam)
   *aham __________ kintu samayaḥ na āsīt.*

2. यदि सः __________ तर्हि सर्वे प्रसन्नाः अभविष्यन्। (had come — supply conditional of āgam)
   *yadi saḥ __________ tarhi sarve prasannāḥ abhaviṣyan.*

3. __________ स्थाने वयं गृहे अस्थिष्याम। (Instead — supply the phrase)
   *__________ sthāne vayaṃ gṛhe asthiṣyāma.*

4. सा अगायिष्यत् __________ कण्ठे पीडा आसीत्। (but — supply the contrastive conjunction)
   *sā agāyiṣyat __________ kaṇṭhe pīḍā āsīt.*

5. __________ अहं तज्ज्ञास्यम् तर्हि अन्यथा अकरिष्यम्। (If — supply the conditional particle)
   *__________ ahaṃ tajjñāsyam tarhi anyathā akariṣyam.*

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks, 3 each)

1. **Prompt:** A friend missed your birthday celebration. They explain what happened using "I would have come but..." and you respond with "If I had known, I would have..." Write 3-4 lines.

2. **Prompt:** Two travelers took a wrong road and ended up somewhere unexpected. One expresses regret using *yadi...tarhi*, the other points out the silver lining of what they did instead (*tat sthāne*). Write 3-4 lines.

---

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks, 2 each)

1. Think of a real situation where your plans changed. Describe what you would have done and what you did instead, in 2-3 Sanskrit sentences.

2. Write a counterfactual about a historical or mythological event: "If X had (not) happened, then Y would have happened." Write 2-3 sentences.

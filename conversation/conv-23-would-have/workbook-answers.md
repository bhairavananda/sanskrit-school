# Stage 23: Would Have / But Instead — Workbook Answers

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit

1. **अहम् आगमिष्यम् किन्तु व्यस्तः आसम्।**
   *aham āgamiṣyam kintu vyastaḥ āsam.*

2. **यदि सा अपठिष्यत् तर्हि उत्तीर्णा अभविष्यत्।**
   *yadi sā apaṭhiṣyat tarhi uttīrṇā abhaviṣyat.*

3. **वयम् तत्र अभक्षयिष्याम किन्तु भोजनालयः पिहितः आसीत्।**
   *vayam tatra abhakṣayiṣyāma kintu bhojanālayaḥ pihitaḥ āsīt.*

4. **तत् स्थाने अहं गृहे पुस्तकम् अपठम्।**
   *tat sthāne ahaṃ gṛhe pustakam apaṭham.*

5. **यदि त्वम् अवदिष्यः तर्हि अहं साहाय्यम् अकरिष्यम्।**
   *yadi tvam avadiṣyaḥ tarhi ahaṃ sāhāyyam akariṣyam.*

---

## Section B: Translate to English

1. I would have bought (it) but there was no money.

2. If it had not rained, we would have played.

3. He would have spoken but he felt fear.

4. Instead, they stayed at home.

5. If the road had been clear, we would have arrived quickly.

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank

1. **अगमिष्यम्** (*agamiṣyam*) — I would have gone (conditional first person of gam)

2. **आगमिष्यत्** (*āgamiṣyat*) — had come / would have come (conditional third person of āgam)

3. **तत्** (*tat*) — full phrase: *tat sthāne* — instead

4. **किन्तु** (*kintu*) — but

5. **यदि** (*yadi*) — if

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue

**1. Missed birthday:**

-- मित्र, क्षम्यताम्। तव जन्मदिने अहम् आगमिष्यम् किन्तु मम यानं भग्नम् अभवत्।
   *mitra, kṣamyatām. tava janmadine aham āgamiṣyam kintu mama yānaṃ bhagnam abhavat.*
   Friend, forgive me. I would have come to your birthday but my vehicle broke down.

-- चिन्ता मास्तु। यदि अहम् अज्ञास्यम् तर्हि यानं प्रेषयिष्यम्।
   *cintā māstu. yadi aham ajñāsyam tarhi yānaṃ preṣayiṣyam.*
   Don't worry. If I had known, I would have sent a vehicle.

-- तत् स्थाने अहं गृहे एव तव कृते मिष्टान्नम् अपचम्!
   *tat sthāne ahaṃ gṛhe eva tava kṛte miṣṭānnam apacam!*
   Instead, I cooked sweets for you at home!

**2. Wrong road silver lining:**

-- हा, यदि वयं मानचित्रं दृष्टवन्तः अभविष्याम तर्हि न भ्रमिष्याम।
   *hā, yadi vayaṃ mānacitram dṛṣṭavantaḥ abhaviṣyāma tarhi na bhramiṣyāma.*
   Alas, if we had looked at the map, we wouldn't have wandered.

-- किन्तु पश्य! तत् स्थाने वयम् एतत् सुन्दरं सरोवरम् अपश्याम!
   *kintu paśya! tat sthāne vayam etat sundaraṃ sarovaram apaśyāma!*
   But look! Instead, we discovered this beautiful lake!

-- सत्यम्। सम्भवति भ्रमणम् एव उत्तमम् आसीत्!
   *satyam. sambhavati bhramaṇam eva uttamam āsīt!*
   True. Perhaps the wandering itself was the best part!

## Section E: Free Response (Sample Answers)

**1.**
अहं मित्रेण सह उद्यानं गन्तुम् ऐच्छम्। अहम् अगमिष्यम् किन्तु वर्षा आगता। तत् स्थाने वयं गृहे चायं पीत्वा कथाम् अवदाम।
*ahaṃ mitreṇa saha udyānaṃ gantum aiccham. aham agamiṣyam kintu varṣā āgatā. tat sthāne vayaṃ gṛhe cāyaṃ pītvā kathām avadāma.*
I wanted to go to the park with a friend. I would have gone but it rained. Instead, we drank tea at home and told stories.

**2.**
यदि रावणः सीतां न अहरिष्यत् तर्हि युद्धं न अभविष्यत्। यदि रामः वनं न अगमिष्यत् तर्हि लङ्कायाः कथा अन्या अभविष्यत्। किन्तु धर्मः एवं विजयम् अलभत।
*yadi rāvaṇaḥ sītāṃ na ahariṣyat tarhi yuddhaṃ na abhaviṣyat. yadi rāmaḥ vanaṃ na agamiṣyat tarhi laṅkāyāḥ kathā anyā abhaviṣyat. kintu dharmaḥ evaṃ vijayam alabhata.*
If Ravana had not abducted Sita, the war would not have happened. If Rama had not gone to the forest, the story of Lanka would have been different. But thus dharma obtained victory.

# Stage 1: This Is / That Is — Workbook Answers

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit

1. etat jalam asti.
2. tat phalam asti.
3. etat kim asti?
4. tat puṣpam asti.
5. etat dugdham na asti.

---

## Section B: Translate to English

1. This is a book.
2. That is a house.
3. What is this?
4. That is a vehicle.
5. This is not a fruit.

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank

1. **etat** pustakam asti.
2. tat **gṛham** asti.
3. etat kim **asti**?
4. tat puṣpam **asti**.
5. etat jalam **na** asti.

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue — Sample Answers

**Dialogue 1 (Kitchen):**

rāmaḥ: etat kim asti?
sītā: etat dugdham asti.
rāmaḥ: etat kim asti?
sītā: etat phalam asti.

**Dialogue 2 (Outside at night):**

bālakaḥ: tat kim asti?
mātā: tat candraḥ asti.
bālakaḥ: etat kim asti?
mātā: etat dīpaḥ asti.

---

## Section E: Free Response — Sample Answers

**1. Room description:**

etat pustakam asti. etat kalamaḥ asti. etat jalam asti.
tat gṛham asti. tat vṛkṣaḥ asti.

**2. Confused person scene:**

bālakaḥ: etat kim asti?
mātā: etat phalam asti.
bālakaḥ: etat kim asti?
mātā: etat puṣpam asti.
bālakaḥ: etat kim asti?
mātā: etat dugdham asti!

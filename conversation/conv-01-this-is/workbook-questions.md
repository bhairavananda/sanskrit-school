# Stage 1: This Is / That Is — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (5 x 2 = 10 marks)

Write each sentence in IAST transliteration.

1. This is water.
2. That is a fruit.
3. What is this?
4. That is a flower.
5. This is not milk.

---

## Section B: Translate to English (5 x 2 = 10 marks)

1. etat pustakam asti.
2. tat gṛham asti.
3. etat kim asti?
4. tat yānam asti.
5. etat phalam na asti.

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (5 x 1 = 5 marks)

1. ______ pustakam asti. (This is a book.)
2. tat ______ asti. (That is a house.)
3. etat kim ______? (What is this?)
4. tat puṣpam ______ . (That is a flower.)
5. etat jalam ______ asti. (This is not water.)

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (2 x 5 = 10 marks)

Write each dialogue in IAST transliteration (3-4 lines each).

1. **Prompt:** Two friends are in a kitchen. One points at various items and asks what they are. The other answers. Use at least two instances of **etat kim asti?**

2. **Prompt:** A child and a parent are outside at night. The child asks about things nearby and far away. Use both **etat** and **tat**.

---

## Section E: Free Response (2 x 2.5 = 5 marks)

1. Look around the room you're in right now. Write three **etat ... asti** sentences about things near you and two **tat ... asti** sentences about things farther away.

2. Write a short (4-5 line) scene where someone is confused about an object and keeps asking **etat kim asti?** before finally getting the right answer.

# Stage 21: I Think & Opinions — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks, 2 each)

1. I think this is a good idea.
2. In my opinion, tea is better than coffee.
3. Certainly he will come tomorrow.
4. It's possible that she is at home.
5. What do you think? (use *kim manyase*)

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks, 2 each)

1. मम मतेन एषः मार्गः सुरक्षितः अस्ति।
   *mama matena eṣaḥ mārgaḥ surakṣitaḥ asti.*

2. अहं मन्ये वयं विजेष्यामः।
   *ahaṃ manye vayaṃ vijeṣyāmaḥ.*

3. सम्भवति अद्य गुरुः न आगमिष्यति।
   *sambhavati adya guruḥ na āgamiṣyati.*

4. निश्चयेन एतत् पुस्तकं पठनीयम् अस्ति।
   *niścayena etat pustakaṃ paṭhanīyam asti.*

5. मम दृष्ट्या परीक्षा कठिना आसीत्।
   *mama dṛṣṭyā parīkṣā kaṭhinā āsīt.*

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks, 2 each)

1. अहं __________ एतत् सत्यम् अस्ति। (I think — use the correct ātmanepada form)
   *ahaṃ __________ etat satyam asti.*

2. __________ मतेन वयं श्वः गच्छामः। (In my opinion)
   *__________ matena vayaṃ śvaḥ gacchāmaḥ.*

3. __________ सा परीक्षायाम् उत्तीर्णा भविष्यति। (Surely)
   *__________ sā parīkṣāyām uttīrṇā bhaviṣyati.*

4. __________ वर्षा न भविष्यति। (It's possible)
   *__________ varṣā na bhaviṣyati.*

5. त्वं किं __________? (What do you think? — supply the ātmanepada verb)
   *tvaṃ kiṃ __________?*

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks, 3 each)

1. **Prompt:** Two friends are debating whether a new restaurant is good or bad. One expresses a positive opinion (*mama matena*), the other politely disagrees (*ahaṃ na evaṃ manye*). Write 3-4 lines.

2. **Prompt:** A student is unsure about an answer on a test. They express uncertainty (*sambhavati*, *syāt*) and a friend expresses certainty (*nūnam*, *niścayena*). Write 3-4 lines.

---

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks, 2 each)

1. Express your opinion about your favorite season. Use *mama matena* and at least one other opinion marker. Write 2-3 sentences in Sanskrit.

2. Write a short exchange where you agree with someone's opinion. Use *satyam eva* or *aham api evaṃ manye*. Write 2-3 sentences.

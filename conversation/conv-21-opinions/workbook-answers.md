# Stage 21: I Think & Opinions — Workbook Answers

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit

1. **अहं मन्ये एतत् उत्तमा युक्तिः अस्ति।**
   *ahaṃ manye etat uttamā yuktiḥ asti.*

2. **मम मतेन चायं कॉफ़ीतः उत्तमा अस्ति।**
   *mama matena cāyaṃ kŏphītaḥ uttamā asti.*

3. **नूनं सः श्वः आगमिष्यति।**
   *nūnaṃ saḥ śvaḥ āgamiṣyati.*

4. **सम्भवति सा गृहे अस्ति।**
   *sambhavati sā gṛhe asti.*

5. **त्वं किं मन्यसे?**
   *tvaṃ kiṃ manyase?*

---

## Section B: Translate to English

1. In my opinion, this road is safe.

2. I think we will win.

3. It's possible the teacher won't come today.

4. Certainly this book should be read.

5. From my perspective, the exam was difficult.

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank

1. **मन्ये** (*manye*) — I think (ātmanepada first person singular)

2. **मम** (*mama*) — my (in my opinion)

3. **नूनम्** (*nūnam*) — surely

4. **सम्भवति** (*sambhavati*) — it's possible

5. **मन्यसे** (*manyase*) — you think (ātmanepada second person singular)

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue

**1. Restaurant debate:**

-- मम मतेन नवीनं भोजनालयं बहु उत्तमम् अस्ति। भोजनं स्वादिष्टम् आसीत्।
   *mama matena navīnaṃ bhojanālayaṃ bahu uttamam asti. bhojanaṃ svādiṣṭam āsīt.*
   In my opinion, the new restaurant is very good. The food was delicious.

-- अहं न एवं मन्ये। मम दृष्ट्या भोजनं लवणाधिकम् आसीत्।
   *ahaṃ na evaṃ manye. mama dṛṣṭyā bhojanaṃ lavaṇādhikam āsīt.*
   I don't think so. From my perspective, the food was too salty.

-- सम्भवति, किन्तु मिष्टान्नं तु उत्तमम् आसीत्, नूनम्!
   *sambhavati, kintu miṣṭānnaṃ tu uttamam āsīt, nūnam!*
   Possibly, but the dessert was excellent, for sure!

**2. Exam uncertainty:**

-- अहं न जानामि मम उत्तरं सम्यक् अस्ति वा न वा। स्यात् अशुद्धम् अस्ति।
   *ahaṃ na jānāmi mama uttaraṃ samyak asti vā na vā. syāt aśuddham asti.*
   I don't know if my answer is correct or not. It might be wrong.

-- नूनं तव उत्तरं शुद्धम् अस्ति। त्वं सम्यक् पठितवान्।
   *nūnaṃ tava uttaraṃ śuddham asti. tvaṃ samyak paṭhitavān.*
   Surely your answer is correct. You studied well.

-- निश्चयेन? सम्भवति त्वं सत्यं वदसि।
   *niścayena? sambhavati tvaṃ satyaṃ vadasi.*
   Certainly? Perhaps you're right.

## Section E: Free Response (Sample Answers)

**1.**
मम मतेन वसन्तः सर्वोत्तमः ऋतुः अस्ति। नूनं पुष्पाणि तस्मिन् काले अतीव सुन्दराणि भवन्ति। मम दृष्ट्या वसन्ते सर्वं जगत् नवीनं भवति।
*mama matena vasantaḥ sarvottamaḥ ṛtuḥ asti. nūnaṃ puṣpāṇi tasmin kāle atīva sundarāṇi bhavanti. mama dṛṣṭyā vasante sarvaṃ jagat navīnaṃ bhavati.*
In my opinion, spring is the best season. Surely the flowers at that time are very beautiful. From my perspective, in spring the whole world becomes new.

**2.**
-- मम मतेन संस्कृतभाषा अतीव मधुरा अस्ति।
   *mama matena saṃskṛtabhāṣā atīva madhurā asti.*
   In my opinion, the Sanskrit language is very sweet.

-- सत्यम् एव! अहम् अपि एवम् एव मन्ये। सा देववाणी अस्ति।
   *satyam eva! aham api evam eva manye. sā devavāṇī asti.*
   Exactly true! I also think exactly so. It is the language of the gods.

# Stage 24: Telling a Story — Workbook Answers

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit

1. **एकदा कस्मिंश्चित् वने एकः सिंहः आसीत्।**
   *ekadā kasmiṃścit vane ekaḥ siṃhaḥ āsīt.*

2. **नदीं दृष्ट्वा सा प्रसन्ना अभवत्।**
   *nadīṃ dṛṣṭvā sā prasannā abhavat.*

3. **"अहं श्वः आगमिष्यामि" इति उक्त्वा सः गृहम् अगच्छत्।**
   *"ahaṃ śvaḥ āgamiṣyāmi" iti uktvā saḥ gṛham agacchat.*

4. **ततः राजा तत् श्रुत्वा कुपितः अभवत्।**
   *tataḥ rājā tat śrutvā kupitaḥ abhavat.*

5. **इति कथा समाप्ता।**
   *iti kathā samāptā.*

---

## Section B: Translate to English

1. Long ago, in a certain city, a queen lived.

2. Having gone to the forest, he climbed a tree.

3. Having said "I am not afraid," she went forward.

4. Having heard that, all the people became happy.

5. Thus that intelligent boy saved everyone.

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank

1. **पुरा** (*purā*) — long ago

2. **श्रुत्वा** (*śrutvā*) — having heard (gerund of śru)

3. **इति** (*iti*) — the quotative particle

4. **ततः** (*tataḥ*) — then

5. **इति** (*iti*) ... **समाप्ता** (*samāptā*) — full phrase: *iti kathā samāptā*

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue

**1. Animal mini-story:**

पुरा कस्मिंश्चित् वने एकः कूर्मः अवसत्। एकदा सः जलात् बहिः गत्वा मार्गे अचलत्। ततः एकं शशकम् दृष्ट्वा सः अवदत् — "मां प्रति धावसि किम्?" इति। शशकः अहसत्। किन्तु कूर्मः मन्दं मन्दं गत्वा प्रथमं लक्ष्यं प्राप्तवान्। एवं धैर्येण कूर्मः विजयम् अलभत। इति कथा समाप्ता।

*purā kasmiṃścit vane ekaḥ kūrmaḥ avasat. ekadā saḥ jalāt bahiḥ gatvā mārge acalat. tataḥ ekaṃ śaśakam dṛṣṭvā saḥ avadat — "māṃ prati dhāvasi kim?" iti. śaśakaḥ ahasat. kintu kūrmaḥ mandaṃ mandaṃ gatvā prathamaṃ lakṣyaṃ prāptavān. evaṃ dhairyeṇa kūrmaḥ vijayam alabhata. iti kathā samāptā.*

Long ago, in a certain forest, a tortoise lived. One day, having come out of the water, he walked on the road. Then, having seen a hare, he said -- "are you racing me?" The hare laughed. But the tortoise, having gone slowly and steadily, reached the goal first. Thus, by patience, the tortoise obtained victory. The story ends here.

**2. What happened yesterday:**

-- ह्यः किम् अभवत्?
   *hyaḥ kim abhavat?*
   What happened yesterday?

-- ह्यः अहं प्रातः उत्थाय उद्यानं गतवान्। तत्र पुष्पाणि दृष्ट्वा प्रसन्नः अभवम्।
   *hyaḥ ahaṃ prātaḥ utthāya udyānaṃ gatavān. tatra puṣpāṇi dṛṣṭvā prasannaḥ abhavam.*
   Yesterday, having woken up in the morning, I went to the garden. Having seen the flowers there, I became happy.

-- ततः किम् अकरोः?
   *tataḥ kim akaroḥ?*
   Then what did you do?

-- ततः गृहम् आगत्य भोजनं पक्त्वा मित्रं आहूतवान्। उत्तमः दिनः आसीत्।
   *tataḥ gṛham āgatya bhojanaṃ paktvā mitraṃ āhūtavān. uttamaḥ dinaḥ āsīt.*
   Then, having come home, having cooked food, I invited a friend. It was an excellent day.

## Section E: Free Response (Sample Answers)

**1. Journey mini-story:**

एकदा एका बालिका पर्वतं द्रष्टुम् ऐच्छत्। "मया एतत् पर्वतम् आरोढव्यम्" इति उक्त्वा सा प्रस्थितवती। मार्गे एकं वृद्धम् दृष्ट्वा सा अवदत् — "पर्वतः कुत्र अस्ति?" इति। सः मार्गं दर्शितवान्। ततः सा बहु श्रमं कृत्वा शिखरं प्राप्तवती। तत्र स्थित्वा सा अमन्यत — "एतत् सर्वोत्तमं दृश्यम् अस्ति।" एवं साहसेन सा सुखं लब्धवती। इति कथा समाप्ता।

*ekadā ekā bālikā parvataṃ draṣṭum aicchat. "mayā etat parvatam āroḍhavyam" iti uktvā sā prasthitavatī. mārge ekaṃ vṛddham dṛṣṭvā sā avadat — "parvataḥ kutra asti?" iti. saḥ mārgaṃ darśitavān. tataḥ sā bahu śramaṃ kṛtvā śikharaṃ prāptavatī. tatra sthitvā sā amanyata — "etat sarvottamaṃ dṛśyam asti." evaṃ sāhasena sā sukhaṃ labdhavatī. iti kathā samāptā.*

Once a girl wanted to see the mountain. Having said "I must climb this mountain," she set out. Having seen an old man on the road, she asked -- "where is the mountain?" He showed the way. Then, having worked very hard, she reached the peak. Standing there, she thought -- "this is the best view." Thus, by courage, she found happiness. The story ends here.

**2. Personal retelling:**

ह्यः अहं विपणिं गतवान्। तत्र बहूनि फलानि दृष्ट्वा आम्राणि क्रीतवान्। ततः गृहम् आगत्य आम्ररसं कृत्वा पीतवान्। एवम् उत्तमः सायङ्कालः आसीत्।

*hyaḥ ahaṃ vipaṇiṃ gatavān. tatra bahūni phalāni dṛṣṭvā āmrāṇi krītavān. tataḥ gṛham āgatya āmrarasaṃ kṛtvā pītavān. evam uttamaḥ sāyaṅkālaḥ āsīt.*

Yesterday I went to the market. Having seen many fruits there, I bought mangoes. Then, having come home, having made mango juice, I drank it. Thus it was an excellent evening.

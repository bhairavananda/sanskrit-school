# Conversational Sanskrit: The Lego Track

A 31-stage parallel track building spoken Sanskrit fluency through phrase patterns.
Each stage is a new "lego piece" — a conversational pattern you learn, play with, and
add to your repertoire. By the end, you can hold a real conversation in Sanskrit.

## How It Works

Each stage introduces **one phrase pattern** — the way Sanskrit expresses a specific
conversational move. You learn the pattern, see it in example dialogues, then practice
building your own sentences with it in the workbook.

The stages are cumulative. By Stage 12, you can handle most daily interactions.
By Stage 24, you can narrate, argue, speculate, and tell stories.
By Stage 30, you sound human — with emphasis, nuance, and conversational repair.

## Four Tiers

| Tier | Stages | What You Get |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Survival** | 1-8 | Navigate basic interactions |
| **Fluency** | 9-16 | Handle real conversations with nuance |
| **Mastery** | 17-24 | Narrate, argue, speculate, persuade |
| **Polish** | 25-31 | Sound human — emphasis, nuance, repair, all 10 lakāras |

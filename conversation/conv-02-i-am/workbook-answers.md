# Stage 2: I Am / You Are — Workbook Answers

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit

1. aham adhyāpakaḥ asmi.
2. tvam sukhī asi? *(or: tvam prasannā asi?)*
3. sā vaidyā asti.
4. aham kṣudhitā asmi.
5. saḥ mama bhrātā asti.

---

## Section B: Translate to English

1. I am a student. (male)
2. Who are you? (to a male)
3. She is a teacher.
4. I am tired. (female)
5. He is happy.

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank

1. aham vaidyaḥ **asmi**.
2. tvam kuśalinī **asi**?
3. **saḥ** adhyāpakaḥ asti.
4. aham **śrāntaḥ** asmi.
5. sā mama **bhaginī** asti.

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue — Sample Answers

**Dialogue 1 (First meeting):**

rāmaḥ: tvam kaḥ asi?
kṛṣṇaḥ: aham kṛṣṇaḥ asmi. aham vaidyaḥ asmi. tvam kaḥ asi?
rāmaḥ: aham rāmaḥ asmi. aham adhyāpakaḥ asmi.

**Dialogue 2 (Visiting friend):**

sītā: tvam kuśalinī asi?
gītā: aham kiñcit śrāntā asmi. saḥ kaḥ asti?
sītā: saḥ mama bhrātā asti. saḥ prasannaḥ asti.

---

## Section E: Free Response — Sample Answers

**1. Self-description:**

aham rāmaḥ asmi. aham chātraḥ asmi. aham prasannaḥ asmi. aham kiñcit kṣudhitaḥ asmi.

**2. Introducing three people:**

eṣaḥ rāmaḥ asti. saḥ vaidyaḥ asti.
eṣā sītā asti. sā adhyāpikā asti.
saḥ kṛṣṇaḥ asti. saḥ chātraḥ asti.

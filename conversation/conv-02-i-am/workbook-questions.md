# Stage 2: I Am / You Are — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (5 x 2 = 10 marks)

Write each sentence in IAST transliteration. Pay attention to the gender indicated.

1. I am a teacher. (male speaker)
2. You are happy. (to a female)
3. She is a doctor.
4. I am hungry. (female speaker)
5. He is my brother.

---

## Section B: Translate to English (5 x 2 = 10 marks)

1. aham chātraḥ asmi.
2. tvam kaḥ asi?
3. sā adhyāpikā asti.
4. aham śrāntā asmi.
5. saḥ prasannaḥ asti.

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (5 x 1 = 5 marks)

1. aham vaidyaḥ ______. (I am a doctor.)
2. tvam kuśalinī ______? (Are you well? — to a female)
3. ______ adhyāpakaḥ asti. (He is a teacher.)
4. aham ______ asmi. (I am tired. — male speaker)
5. sā mama ______ asti. (She is my sister.)

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (2 x 5 = 10 marks)

Write each dialogue in IAST transliteration (3-4 lines each).

1. **Prompt:** Two people meet for the first time. They introduce themselves by name and occupation. One asks "Who are you?" Use **aham ... asmi** and **tvam kaḥ/kā asi?**

2. **Prompt:** A friend visits and asks how you are. You say you're tired. You ask about a third person (he/she). Use at least one instance each of **asmi**, **asi**, and **asti**.

---

## Section E: Free Response (2 x 2.5 = 5 marks)

1. Describe yourself in four Sanskrit sentences using **aham ... asmi**. Include your name, one occupation or role, and two states (happy, tired, hungry, ready, etc.).

2. Write a scene (4-5 lines) where someone introduces three people at a gathering, using **saḥ ... asti** and **sā ... asti**.

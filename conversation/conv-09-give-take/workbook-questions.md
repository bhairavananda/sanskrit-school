# Stage 9: Give & Take — Workbook

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)
*Write in IAST.*

1. Give me water.
2. I give you this fruit.
3. Take this book.
4. Please give her a flower. (polite)
5. What shall I give you?

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. mahyam ekaṃ cāyaṃ dehi.
2. tubhyam etat pustakaṃ dadāmi.
3. tasmai dhanaṃ dehi.
4. etat sarvaṃ gṛhāṇa.
5. tasyai puṣpaṃ dadātu.

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)
*Fill in the missing word.*

1. ______ jalaṃ dehi. (Give me water.)
2. tubhyam etat phalaṃ ______. (I give you this fruit.)
3. etat pustakaṃ ______. (Take this book.)
4. ______ dhanaṃ dehi. (Give him money.)
5. ahaṃ tubhyaṃ kiṃ ______? (What shall I give you?)

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)
*Write a 3-4 line dialogue in IAST for each prompt.*

1. You are at a tea stall. Order tea, receive it, and pay. Use dehi, gṛhāṇa, and the dative.

2. A friend's birthday: you offer a gift ("what shall I give you?"), they ask for a book, you hand it to them with gṛhāṇa.

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks)

1. Describe a market scene where two people exchange items — use at least three give/take phrases (3-4 sentences in IAST).

2. Write a polite request to a teacher for something you need, using dadātu (3-4 sentences in IAST).

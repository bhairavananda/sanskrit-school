# Stage 9: Give & Take — Answer Key

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks)

1. mahyam jalaṃ dehi.
2. tubhyam etat phalaṃ dadāmi.
3. etat pustakaṃ gṛhāṇa.
4. tasyai puṣpaṃ dadātu.
5. tubhyam kiṃ dadāmi?

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks)

1. Give me one tea.
2. I give you this book.
3. Give him money.
4. Take all of this.
5. Please give her a flower.

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks)

1. **mahyam** jalaṃ dehi.
2. tubhyam etat phalaṃ **dadāmi**.
3. etat pustakaṃ **gṛhāṇa**.
4. **tasmai** dhanaṃ dehi.
5. ahaṃ tubhyaṃ kiṃ **dadāmi**?

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (6 marks)

### Dialogue 1 (sample)
> A: mahyam ekaṃ cāyaṃ dehi.
> B: etat gṛhāṇa. pañcarūpyakāṇi dehi.
> A: etat gṛhāṇa. dhanyavādaḥ!
> B: svāgatam.

### Dialogue 2 (sample)
> A: adya tava janmadinam! tubhyam kiṃ dadāmi?
> B: mahyam ekaṃ pustakaṃ dehi.
> A: etat pustakaṃ gṛhāṇa. janmadinaśubhāśayāḥ!
> B: dhanyavādaḥ!

## Section E: Free Response (4 marks)

### Sample 1
āpaṇe ekaḥ janaḥ vadati — "mahyam phalāni dehi." āpaṇikaḥ vadati — "etat gṛhāṇa. viṃśatirūpyakāṇi dehi." janaḥ dhanaṃ dadāti. āpaṇikaḥ vadati — "dhanyavādaḥ!"

### Sample 2
ācārya, mahyam ekaṃ pustakaṃ dadātu. saṃskṛtasya pustakam āvaśyakam. mama pustakam na asti. kṛpayā dadātu.

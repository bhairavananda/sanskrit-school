# Stage 14: Because & Therefore — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (10 marks, 2 each)

Use any causal construction from this stage (yataḥ...tataḥ, -kāraṇāt, ataḥ, hi).

1. Because it is cold, I wear warm clothes.
2. He is tired, therefore he sleeps.
3. Because of hunger, the child cries.
4. For knowledge is the greatest wealth.
5. Because you studied, therefore you passed.

---

## Section B: Translate to English (10 marks, 2 each)

1. yataḥ sūryaḥ astam gacchati tataḥ andhakāraḥ bhavati.
2. jalābhāva-kāraṇāt vṛkṣāḥ śuṣyanti.
3. sā dhīrā, ataḥ na bibheti.
4. jīvanaṃ hi anityam.
5. yataḥ tvaṃ satyaṃ vadasi tataḥ ahaṃ viśvasimi.

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (10 marks, 2 each)

1. _______ varṣā bhavati _______ nadī pūrṇā bhavati. (Because it rains, therefore the river fills.)
2. śrama-_______ saḥ viśrāmyati. (Because of exhaustion, he rests.)
3. vidyā _______ sarvōttamā. (For knowledge is the highest.)
4. mārgaḥ dūraḥ, _______ vayaṃ yānaṃ svīkurmāḥ. (The road is long, therefore we take a vehicle.)
5. _______ guruḥ kathayati _______ śiṣyaḥ śṛṇoti. (Because the teacher speaks, therefore the student listens.)

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (5 marks, 2.5 each)

Write a 3-4 line dialogue in Sanskrit (IAST is fine) for each situation. Use at least one causal construction per dialogue.

1. **Situation:** Your friend asks why you are learning Sanskrit. Give a reason using *yataḥ...tataḥ* or *hi*.

2. **Situation:** Someone asks why the shop is closed. Explain the reason using *-kāraṇāt* and conclude with *ataḥ*.

---

## Section E: Free Response (5 marks, 2.5 each)

1. Write 3-4 sentences in Sanskrit explaining why you like (or dislike) a particular season. Use at least two different causal constructions.

2. The word *hi* always comes second in a clause. Why do you think Sanskrit has this rule? Write 2-3 sentences reflecting — in English or Sanskrit.

# Stage 5: Doing Things

**Pattern Name:** aham X karomi / tvam kim karoṣi?

---

## The Pattern

| Sanskrit | IAST | Meaning |
|----------|------|---------|
| अहम् X करोमि | aham X karomi | I do X |
| त्वम् किम् करोषि? | tvam kim karoṣi? | What are you doing? |
| सः Y पठति | saḥ Y paṭhati | He reads Y |

This is your action toolkit. One verb at a time, you can describe everything anyone is doing right now.

---

## How It Works

Sanskrit present tense verbs change their ending based on who's doing the action. The pattern for most common verbs: **-āmi** (I), **-asi** (you), **-ati** (he/she/it). So **paṭhati** (he reads) becomes **paṭhāmi** (I read) and **paṭhasi** (you read). The verb **karoti** ("does/makes") is your all-purpose action word — it's slightly irregular: **karomi**, **karoṣi**, **karoti**.

Here are eight high-frequency verbs to get you going:

| Verb | IAST | Meaning | I (aham) | You (tvam) | He/She (saḥ/sā) |
|------|------|---------|----------|------------|-----------------|
| करोति | karoti | does | karomi | karoṣi | karoti |
| पठति | paṭhati | reads | paṭhāmi | paṭhasi | paṭhati |
| खादति | khādati | eats | khādāmi | khādasi | khādati |
| पिबति | pibati | drinks | pibāmi | pibasi | pibati |
| वदति | vadati | speaks | vadāmi | vadasi | vadati |
| लिखति | likhati | writes | likhāmi | likhasi | likhati |
| गच्छति | gacchati | goes | gacchāmi | gacchasi | gacchati |
| पश्यति | paśyati | sees | paśyāmi | paśyasi | paśyati |

---

## Example Phrases

1. अहम् पुस्तकम् पठामि।
   aham pustakam paṭhāmi.
   "I read a book."

2. त्वम् किम् करोषि?
   tvam kim karoṣi?
   "What are you doing?"

3. सः भोजनम् खादति।
   saḥ bhojanam khādati.
   "He eats food."

4. सा जलम् पिबति।
   sā jalam pibati.
   "She drinks water."

5. अहम् पत्रम् लिखामि।
   aham patram likhāmi.
   "I write a letter."

6. त्वम् कुत्र गच्छसि?
   tvam kutra gacchasi?
   "Where are you going?"

7. सः संस्कृतम् वदति।
   saḥ saṃskṛtam vadati.
   "He speaks Sanskrit."

8. अहम् वृक्षम् पश्यामि।
   aham vṛkṣam paśyāmi.
   "I see a tree."

9. सा विद्यालयम् गच्छति।
   sā vidyālayam gacchati.
   "She goes to school."

10. त्वम् किम् खादसि?
    tvam kim khādasi?
    "What are you eating?"

## Mini-Dialogue 1

**रामः (rāmaḥ):** त्वम् किम् करोषि?
tvam kim karoṣi?
"What are you doing?"

**सीता (sītā):** अहम् पुस्तकम् पठामि। त्वम् किम् करोषि?
aham pustakam paṭhāmi. tvam kim karoṣi?
"I'm reading a book. What are you doing?"

**रामः:** अहम् पत्रम् लिखामि।
aham patram likhāmi.
"I'm writing a letter."

**सीता:** कम् प्रति लिखसि?
kam prati likhasi?
"To whom are you writing?"

## Mini-Dialogue 2

**माता (mātā):** बालकः कुत्र गच्छति?
bālakaḥ kutra gacchati?
"Where is the boy going?"

**पिता (pitā):** सः विद्यालयम् गच्छति।
saḥ vidyālayam gacchati.
"He is going to school."

**माता:** सा किम् करोति?
sā kim karoti?
"What is she doing?"

**पिता:** सा भोजनम् खादति।
sā bhojanam khādati.
"She is eating food."

---

## Variations

1. **Adding "also" (api):** Slip **api** after the word it modifies: **aham api gacchāmi** = "I also go." **sā api paṭhati** = "She also reads."

2. **Negation with na:** Place **na** before the verb: **aham na gacchāmi** = "I am not going." **saḥ na vadati** = "He does not speak."

3. **Habitual vs. right now:** The present tense covers both habits and current actions. **aham pratidinām paṭhāmi** = "I read every day." Context tells you which is meant.

---

## Common Mistakes

1. **Mixing up karoṣi and karosi.** The "you" form of **karoti** has a retroflex **ṣ**: **karoṣi**, not **karosi**. Most other verbs use regular **-asi**.

2. **Forgetting accusative on the object.** The thing being acted upon takes accusative: **pustakam paṭhāmi** (reading a book-ACC), not **pustakaḥ paṭhāmi**.

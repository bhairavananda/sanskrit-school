## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue — Sample Answers

**Dialogue 1 (Two friends):**

rāmaḥ: tvam kim karoṣi?
kṛṣṇaḥ: aham pustakam paṭhāmi. tvam kim karoṣi?
rāmaḥ: aham patram likhāmi. sītā kutra gacchati?
kṛṣṇaḥ: sā vidyālayam gacchati.

**Dialogue 2 (Parent and child):**

mātā: tvam kim paśyasi?
bālakaḥ: aham vṛkṣam paśyāmi.
mātā: tvam kim khādasi?
bālakaḥ: aham phalam khādāmi.
mātā: tvam kutra gacchasi?
bālakaḥ: aham vidyālayam gacchāmi.

---

## Section E: Free Response — Sample Answers

**1. Typical morning:**

aham prātaḥ jalam pibāmi. aham bhojanam khādāmi. aham pustakam paṭhāmi. aham vidyālayam gacchāmi.

**2. Family scene:**

saḥ pitā pustakam paṭhati. sā mātā bhojanam karoti. saḥ bhrātā jalam pibati. sā bhaginī patram likhati.

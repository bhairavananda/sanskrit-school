# Stage 5: Doing Things — Workbook Questions

**Total: 40 marks**

---

## Section A: Translate to Sanskrit (5 x 2 = 10 marks)

Write each sentence in IAST transliteration.

1. I read a book.
2. What are you doing?
3. She drinks water.
4. He goes to school.
5. I am writing a letter.

---

## Section B: Translate to English (5 x 2 = 10 marks)

1. saḥ bhojanam khādati.
2. tvam kutra gacchasi?
3. aham saṃskṛtam vadāmi.
4. sā pustakam paṭhati.
5. tvam kim paśyasi?

---

## Section C: Fill in the Blank (5 x 1 = 5 marks)

Fill in the correct verb form.

1. aham pustakam ______. (I read a book.) [paṭh-]
2. tvam kutra ______? (Where are you going?) [gacch-]
3. saḥ jalam ______. (He drinks water.) [pib-]
4. aham patram ______. (I write a letter.) [likh-]
5. sā bhojanam ______. (She eats food.) [khād-]

---

## Section D: Build a Mini-Dialogue (2 x 5 = 10 marks)

Write each dialogue in IAST transliteration (3-4 lines each).

1. **Prompt:** Two friends ask each other what they are doing. One is reading, the other is writing. They also ask where someone else (a third person) is going. Use at least three different verbs.

2. **Prompt:** A parent asks a child what they see, what they are eating, and where they are going. The child answers each question. Use **paśyati**, **khādati**, and **gacchati**.

---

## Section E: Free Response (2 x 2.5 = 5 marks)

1. Describe your typical morning in four Sanskrit sentences using four different verbs from this stage.

2. Write a scene (4-5 lines) describing what different family members are doing right now, using **saḥ/sā ... verb** for each person.

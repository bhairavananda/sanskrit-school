# Stage 1 Badge: Nāmavit

**Title:** Word Collector
**Badge:** 🏅 Nāmavit

## Requirements

Post a list of 20 Sanskrit nouns (deity names, pūjā items, sacred objects) with correct gender marker and English meaning.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post in #badge-submissions

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 2 content and grants the **Nāmavit** role on Discord.

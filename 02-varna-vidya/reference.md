# Varṇa-Vidyā — Reference Guide

## The Five Sthānas (Places of Articulation)

| Sthāna | Location | Element | Letters Produced |
|--------|----------|---------|-----------------|
| Kaṇṭhya | Throat | Ākāśa | a, ā, ka-varga, ha, visarga |
| Tālavya | Hard palate | Vāyu | i, ī, ca-varga, ya, śa |
| Mūrdhanya | Dome of palate | Agni | ṛ, ṝ, ṭa-varga, ra, ṣa |
| Dantya | Teeth | Āpas | ḷ, ḹ, ta-varga, la, sa |
| Oṣṭhya | Lips | Pṛthivī | u, ū, pa-varga, va (danta-oṣṭhya) |
| Kaṇṭha-tālu | Throat+palate | — | e, ai |
| Kaṇṭha-oṣṭha | Throat+lips | — | o, au |
| Nāsikā | Nose | — | ṃ (anusvāra), all nasals |

## Complete Varṇamālā (48/50 letters)

### Svarāḥ (16 vowels)
a ā i ī u ū ṛ ṝ ḷ ḹ e ai o au aṃ aḥ

### Sparśāḥ (25 stops)
ka kha ga gha ṅa | ca cha ja jha ña | ṭa ṭha ḍa ḍha ṇa | ta tha da dha na | pa pha ba bha ma

### Antasthāḥ (4 semivowels)
ya ra la va

### Ūṣmāṇaḥ (4 fricatives/aspirate)
śa ṣa sa ha

### Additional (in some counts)
kṣa (ka + ṣa), jña (ja + ña)

## Key Pratyāhāras

| Pratyāhāra | = | Used in |
|------------|---|---------|
| **ac** | All vowels (a through au) | Vowel sandhi rules |
| **hal** | All consonants (ha through ha) | Consonant identification |
| **ik** | i, u, ṛ, ḷ | Guṇa/vṛddhi rules |
| **aṇ** | a, i, u | Basic vowel triad |
| **eṅ** | e, o | Simple diphthongs |
| **ec** | e, o, ai, au | All diphthongs |
| **yar** | All sonorants + voiced stops | Sandhi contexts |
| **jaś** | ja, ba, ga, ḍa, da | 3rd-column voiced stops |
| **khar** | All voiceless consonants + sibilants | Voiceless environment rules |

## Bīja Anatomy Quick Reference

### Architecture: Consonant(s) + Vowel + Bindu

| Component | Role | Common Values |
|-----------|------|--------------|
| Consonant(s) | Form/sthāna of the energy | ka, ha, śa, kla, etc. |
| Vowel | Life-force | ī (śakti), ū (stability), ai (speech), au (fullness) |
| Bindu (ṃ) | Seal — sound collapses into the point | Always anusvāra |
| Visarga (ḥ) | Breath-seal — exhaled, not collapsed | In sauḥ, namaḥ |

### The ra-la Distinction in Bījas

| Bīja | Middle letter | Sthāna | Element | Function |
|------|-------------|--------|---------|----------|
| **Krīṃ** (Kālī) | ra | Mūrdhan | Fire | Burns, transforms, liberates |
| **Klīṃ** (Kāma) | la | Danta | Earth | Attracts, grounds, embodies |
| **Hrīṃ** (Māyā) | ra | Mūrdhan | Fire | Veils and reveals through fire |
| **Hlīṃ** (variant) | la | Danta | Earth | Grounds the māyā principle |

### Bālā Mantra Components (Aiṃ Klīṃ Sauḥ)

| Bīja | Kūṭa | Śakti | Letter Analysis |
|------|-------|-------|----------------|
| Aiṃ | Vāgbhava | Jñāna | ai (intensified a+i union at speech-level) + ṃ |
| Klīṃ | Kāmarāja | Icchā | ka (impulse) + la (earth-descent) + ī (śakti-matrix) + ṃ |
| Sauḥ | Śakti | Kriyā | sa (tat) + au (union/samarasya) + ḥ (aham/visarga) |

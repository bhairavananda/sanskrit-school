# Stage 2 Badge: Varṇajña

**Title:** Letter Master
**Badge:** 🏅 Varṇajña

## Requirements

1. Recite all 14 Māheśvara Sūtras from memory (voice note)
2. Dissect 3 bīja mantras letter-by-letter (sthāna + prayatna for each varṇa)
3. Expand 3 pratyāhāras (e.g., ac, hal, yaṇ) into their component letters

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Voice note + text post

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 3 content and grants the **Varṇajña** role on Discord.

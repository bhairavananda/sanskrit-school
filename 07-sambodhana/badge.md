# Stage 8 Badge: Prārthanākāra

**Title:** Prayer Caller
**Badge:** 🏅 Prārthanākāra

## Requirements

1. Compose and recite a spontaneous 4-line prayer using vocative + imperatives
2. Give sambodhana forms for 5 nouns across different baseplates

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Voice note + text

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 9 content and grants the **Prārthanākāra** role on Discord.

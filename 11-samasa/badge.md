# Stage 11 Badge: Samāsakāra

**Title:** Compound Builder
**Badge:** 🏅 Samāsakāra

## Requirements

1. Create 5 new grammatically valid compound epithets and classify each (tatpuruṣa/bahuvrīhi/dvandva/etc.)
2. Break down 5 given compound words into their components
3. Identify sandhi at compound junctions

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 12 content and grants the **Samāsakāra** role on Discord.

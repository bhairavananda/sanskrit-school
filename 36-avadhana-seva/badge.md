# Stage 36 Badge: Avadhānī

**Title:** Master Builder

Perform a full Aṣṭāvadhāna before a live audience. This badge is granted by community consensus after a public performance.

**Format:** Live performance — community vote

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 37 content and grants the **Avadhānī** role on Discord.

## Section E: Āvaraṇa Bīja Analysis (15 marks)

**E1.** The āvaraṇa bījas show a progressive "h-infusion." Trace the derivation (5 marks):

| Āvaraṇa | Bīja | What prefix was added? |
|----------|------|----------------------|
| 2nd | aiṃ klīṃ sauḥ | (base — no prefix) |
| 4th | haiṃ hklīṃ hsauḥ | ? |
| 5th | hsaiṃ hsklīṃ hssauḥ | ? |

What does "ha" represent in the tantric context? What does the progression from no-prefix → h- → hs- signify?

**E2.** Between the 2nd and 3rd āvaraṇas, aiṃ changes to hrīṃ while klīṃ and sauḥ remain the same. What śakti does aiṃ represent? What śakti does hrīṃ represent? Why does this substitution happen as one moves deeper into the Śrīcakra? (5 marks)

**E3.** The principle of h-infusion in the āvaraṇa bījas is compared to upasarga-prefixation in Sanskrit grammar (e.g., √gam → āgam → samāgam). Explain this analogy: how is adding "h-" to a bīja similar to adding an upasarga to a dhātu? (5 marks)

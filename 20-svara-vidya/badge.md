# Stage 20 Badge: Svaravit

**Title:** Accent Keeper
**Badge:** 🏅 Svaravit

## Requirements

1. Mark udātta, anudātta, and svarita on one complete Rudram anuvāka
2. Explain the 3 svara types and their marking convention
3. Demonstrate one vikṛti (krama or jaṭā) for 3 words

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text + voice note

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 21 content and grants the **Svaravit** role on Discord.

# Stage 19 Badge: Chandasvit I

**Title:** Meter Listener
**Badge:** 🏅 Chandasvit I

## Requirements

1. Identify the chandas (meter) of 5 given ślokas
2. Mark laghu (u) and guru (—) for 3 pādas
3. Explain the structure of anuṣṭubh

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 20 content and grants the **Chandasvit I** role on Discord.

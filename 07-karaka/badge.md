# Stage 7 Badge: Kārakavit

**Title:** Sentence Architect
**Badge:** 🏅 Kārakavit

## Requirements

1. Construct 5 complete sentences, each using a different kāraka as the focus
2. Label all kārakas in a given 3-line Sanskrit passage
3. Rearrange one sentence 3 ways showing free word order

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 8 content and grants the **Kārakavit** role on Discord.

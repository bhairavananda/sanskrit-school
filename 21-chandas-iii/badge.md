# Stage 22 Badge: Chandasvit III

**Title:** Multi-Meter Poet
**Badge:** 🏅 Chandasvit III

## Requirements

Compose one verse each in 3 different meters (e.g., anuṣṭubh, indravajrā, vasantatilakā). Show the scansion.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post with scansion

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 23 content and grants the **Chandasvit III** role on Discord.

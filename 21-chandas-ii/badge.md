# Stage 21 Badge: Chandasvit II

**Title:** Anuṣṭubh Composer
**Badge:** 🏅 Chandasvit II

## Requirements

Compose an original anuṣṭubh verse (32 syllables, 4 pādas of 8). Must follow pathyā pattern in 2nd and 4th pādas.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post with scansion

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 22 content and grants the **Chandasvit II** role on Discord.

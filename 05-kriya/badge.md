# Stage 6 Badge: Kriyāvit

**Title:** Verb Builder
**Badge:** 🏅 Kriyāvit

## Requirements

1. Conjugate 3 dhātus in laṭ lakāra (all 9 parasmaipada forms)
2. Conjugate 1 dhātu in lṛṭ lakāra
3. Write 5 sentences describing your own worship using correct verb forms

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Text post

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 7 content and grants the **Kriyāvit** role on Discord.

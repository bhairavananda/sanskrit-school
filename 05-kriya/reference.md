## Imperative (Loṭ-lakāra) — Quick Reference

| Person | Eka | Meaning |
|--------|-----|---------|
| 3rd | -tu | let him... |
| 2nd | -a / -hi | do...! |
| 1st | -āni | let me... |

Examples: pātu (let him protect), gaccha (go!), karāṇi (let me do)

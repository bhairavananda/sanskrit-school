# Stage 4: Kriyā — Verbs

## Goal
Learn Sanskrit verbs to describe acts of worship, devotion, and daily spiritual practice. Describe your own worship in Sanskrit.

## What is a Kriyā?

**Kriyā** (क्रिया) means "action." Sanskrit verbs conjugate according to:
- **Puruṣa** (person): prathama (3rd), madhyama (2nd), uttama (1st)
- **Vacana** (number): eka (singular), dvi (dual), bahu (plural)
- **Lakāra** (tense/mood): laṭ (present), loṭ (imperative), laṅ (past), etc.

## Present Tense (Laṭ-lakāra) — The Essential Tense

### Parasmaipada (active) endings:

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| Prathama (he/she/it) | -ti | -taḥ | -anti |
| Madhyama (you) | -si | -thaḥ | -tha |
| Uttama (I) | -mi | -vaḥ | -maḥ |

## All Three Persons

Example with "nam" (to bow):

| Person | Singular | Meaning |
|--------|----------|---------|
| 3rd | namati | he/she bows |
| 2nd | namasi | you bow |
| 1st | namāmi | I bow |

Example with "pūj" (to worship, causative/10th class):

| Person | Singular | Meaning |
|--------|----------|---------|
| 3rd | pūjayati | he/she worships |
| 2nd | pūjayasi | you worship |
| 1st | pūjayāmi | I worship |

## Ātmanepada Verbs

Some verbs use ātmanepada (middle voice) endings:

| Person | Singular | Dual | Plural |
|--------|----------|------|--------|
| 3rd | -te | -ete | -ante |
| 2nd | -se | -ethe | -dhve |
| 1st | -e | -vahe | -mahe |

Key ātmanepada verb: **sev** (to serve) → **seve** (I serve)

## The Game: "What Do I Offer?"

Describe your daily worship using verbs:

> prātaḥ uttiṣṭhāmi (in the morning I rise)
> snānaṃ karomi (I bathe)
> mandiram gacchāmi (I go to the temple)
> devam paśyāmi (I see the deity)
> puṣpam arpayāmi (I offer a flower)
> dhūpam dadāmi (I give incense)
> dīpam jvālayāmi (I light a lamp)
> mantram japāmi (I chant a mantra)
> stotram gāyāmi (I sing a hymn)
> devam namāmi (I bow to the deity)
> dhyāyāmi (I meditate)
> prasādam svīkaromi (I accept prasāda)

## Negation

To negate a verb, place **na** before it:
- na jānāmi — I do not know
- na paśyāmi — I do not see
- na tyajāmi — I do not abandon

## Milestone Check

Can you describe your own daily worship (real or ideal) using at least 8 different verbs?

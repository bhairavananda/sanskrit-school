# Stage 18 Badge: Kathāvācaka

**Title:** Story Teller
**Badge:** 🏅 Kathāvācaka

## Requirements

Narrate a 2-3 minute story in Sanskrit (a purāṇic episode or original). Must use past, present, and future tenses.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Voice recording

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 19 content and grants the **Kathāvācaka** role on Discord.

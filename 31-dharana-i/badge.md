# Stage 31 Badge: Dhāraṇāvit I

**Title:** Memory Holder
**Badge:** 🏅 Dhāraṇāvit I

## Requirements

Begin composing a verse, get interrupted with 3 unrelated questions, answer them, then complete the verse correctly.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Live in voice channel

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 32 content and grants the **Dhāraṇāvit I** role on Discord.

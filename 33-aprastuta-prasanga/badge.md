# Stage 33 Badge: Vāgmin

**Title:** Wit Under Fire
**Badge:** 🏅 Vāgmin

## Requirements

While composing a verse, respond to 5 witty/distracting interruptions with relevant Sanskrit humor, then complete the verse.

## How to Demonstrate

**Format:** Live in voice channel

Post your submission in **#badge-submissions** on Discord. A reviewer will verify and grant the badge.

## Unlock

Completing this badge unlocks Stage 34 content and grants the **Vāgmin** role on Discord.
